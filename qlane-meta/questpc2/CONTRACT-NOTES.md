# questpc2 n3 decisions

- Keep the existing chosen recipes. In particular, Pits of Inferno omits source vocation/sacrifice/timing puzzles, and 25 Years reports to Lord Retro rather than requiring a boss fight.
- Enforce chosen prerequisites at admission, rather than only at final reward. For Twenty Miles, interpret Helmet of the Deep as equipped head gear for the underwater mission, not merely an item carried in a bag.
- Preserve Tower Defence's four distinct fifty-arrow grants as one completion transaction. Do not invent a reward container or turn one reward into four separately acknowledged commands.
- Keep source NPC topics and effects under one fenced owner. No workaround may compose an unrelated reward-callback conversation with stale source-NPC confirmation state.
- Existing qualified QENGINE2 authored bindings, W02 admission and W10 loot must be reused. Remaining shared dependencies are listed in ENGINE-REQUESTS.md; resolving a dependency does not qualify its quest automatically.
- Previous end-to-end proofs remain historical evidence at their original pushed SHAs. This branch's audit neither reruns those cases nor claims their unmerged implementations are present on main.
