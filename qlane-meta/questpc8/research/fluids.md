## Dreamer’s Challenge

- **Wiki scope and access:** The main page marks the quest premium-only, recommends level 40, and locates it at the Abandoned Monastery and under the Plains of Havoc in the Dream Realm. It describes faction membership and rewards. [TIBIAWIKI_STRUCTURED: Dreamer’s Challenge Quest, page 12354, revid 869211]
- **Entry route and equipment:** The spoiler lists rope, shovel, pick, life ring, 1–3 water containers, 1–2 fire field runes, two death rings, fire bug, and carrot; orichalcum pearls are advised for teleport exits. Go to the Plains of Havoc temple, enter the ruined building east of it (mapper coordinates `128.69|125.253|7|4`), shovel the northeast hole, pick until another hole appears, pass two ghouls, use a carrot at the tree teleport (free-account players cannot pass), cross poison fields, use a fire bug on the empty tile, then throw a death ring on the switch to clear the trees and enter before the teleport closes. [TIBIAWIKI_STRUCTURED: Dreamer’s Challenge Quest/Spoiler, page 12355, revid 891457]
- **Puzzle sequence:** First match four colors with switches, then take the right teleport. In the lever-and-wall room, pull switches SE, SW, NE, NW while the 12 ominous pillars deal increasing damage, then enter the teleport after all walls fall. For the 6×6 tic-tac-toe board, use the Book of the Lost Heroes’ six names and place only the X/O letters (`Askuro`, `Mantar`, `Ildoni`, `Maxmof`, `Xemodo`, `Oxanti`); move the next switch twice to restore pillars and open the teleport. [TIBIAWIKI_STRUCTURED: Dreamer’s Challenge Quest/Spoiler, page 12355, revid 891457]
- **Dream-room access and boxes:** Put a life ring on the north tile and death ring on the south tile; use the northwest switch to fill the well with slime and turn on electricity; pour water on the west tile and shoot a fire-field rune at the east tile. The center teleport opens. The subsequent dreamwalk ends at A Ghostly Guardian; all four boxes there contain a bag with a vial of wine, bread, and certificate, after which the player chooses a faction teleport and speaks to its leader to join. [TIBIAWIKI_STRUCTURED: Dreamer’s Challenge Quest/Spoiler, page 12355, revid 891457]

## Fluid subtype choice

- **Choose the wiki’s named reward, encode with donor enum:** Goblin’s current wiki page names milk and Dreamer’s spoiler names wine. Crystal and Canary instead give vial 2874 raw argument 1. Crystal passes that argument to `reward:addItem`, the container API forwards it to item construction, and the constructor stores it as `FLUIDTYPE` for fluid containers. Crystal’s enum maps 1 to water, 2 to wine, and 9 to milk. Therefore the accepted recipe semantics should be milk (donor enum 9) for Goblin and wine (donor enum 2) for Dreamer; the donor raw 1 is a copied water value and loses to the explicit wiki names. This choice uses donor encoding semantics, not a claim about CipSoft’s numeric representation. ## Present

- **Wiki contents and exchange:** The Present Quest page lists a backpack containing a jug, plate, cup, and present or Legion Helmet; it gives no fluid content for the jug or cup. The spoiler says the chest is near the Bear Room Quest room and a switch; take the backpack, keep the present, go to Seymour (mapper coordinates `125.102|125.196|7|3`) and say `box`. The transcript has Seymour ask whether the player has a suitable present box; after `yes`, he gives a helmet. The page says only one present can be obtained on Rookgaard. [TIBIAWIKI_STRUCTURED: Present Quest, page 3214, revid 1044890; Present Quest/Spoiler, page 5480, revid 749066]
- **Donor contents:** Crystal UID 6113 at `(32149,32105,11)` grants backpack 2854 with jug 2882, present 2856, cup 2881, and plate 2905, each raw argument 1, under `useKV=true`, key `presentBox`. [Crystal:data-global/startup/tables/chest.lua:1447-1456]
- **Chosen donor fill behavior:** Crystal registers item IDs 2881 and 2882 for its fluid action. In that donor constructor, a fluid-container item takes the second argument as `FLUIDTYPE`, and enum 1 is water; accordingly the donor interpretation is water in both the jug and cup. The pinned XML labels them “cup” and “jug” under kitchen tools, while the wiki is silent about liquid; treat water as a Crystal-runtime inference, not a wiki/CipSoft fact.
