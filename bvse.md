2. **P1 — Extra action-bar bindings use the wrong storage paths.** `apps/client/src/bevy_shell/game_ui/options/keys.rs:105` and `:153`. For every `local-spell-action-bar-spell-5` through `-450`, select the action through General Hotkeys and assign a primary chord. Set Secondary also calls the ordinary secondary-map API, whose validation rejects every extra-slot ID. Route these operations through storage appropriate to action-bar slots, or disable unsupported controls.
