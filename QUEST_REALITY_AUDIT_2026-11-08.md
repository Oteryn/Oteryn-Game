## Result and decisions

**Five current Oteryn quest programmes have accepted real-dispatch start→finish proofs. Another 64 titles have at least one physically bound or admitted conditional runtime path without an accepted whole-programme proof; 35 have only served reward-chest paths; 263 need bindings/data; six require the quest mount-grant adapter as well as bindings.** All 373 title rows and 352 definitions are accounted for below. These are code/binding classifications under the committed non-production world pin, not a claim of Source-faithful Tibia campaigns or observed live-server availability. The actual deployed build/pins, daily state and public-spawn traversal were not observed; those facts remain **unknown**.

Recorded decisions:
- Use executable native programmes where present; for an unbound authoring record, inspect its explicitly selected Oteryn finite recipe. Readiness/runtime_enabled/work_state annotations never decide a class. A chosen approximation is not the full original Tibia campaign.
- Class precedence: an accepted complete proof with real current physical NPC bindings; otherwise a positively verified mandatory mechanic/adapter gap; otherwise an actual static/conditional programme route; otherwise a served chest; otherwise supported but unbound recipe/content. LIVE_UNPROVEN expressly includes partial routes and does not promise a finish.
- Normal initial inventory/level/entitlements are test inputs, not proofs of Source loot acquisition. Foreign quest-progress fixtures disqualify a whole-programme proof in this audit. Citizen Rook’s timestamp-only wait arrangement is accepted because the test first proves early refusal and asserts every non-timer track unchanged (`crates/gameplay/dispatch/src/gameplay_transport/postgres_tests/qengine_outfit_stories.rs:1496`, :1520). It is not a real-time scheduler or public-spawn journey proof.
- Fixture-created NPCs do not establish a live quest. Every mandatory NPC of an accepted proof must also have a current admitted static placement and a production talk-book entry. A loaded selector with no current source is not LIVE. A runtime projector counts as conditional binding only when its actual admission and creation caller exist.
- Shared/family canonical identities do not automatically certify every named member. Explicit outfit grant targets, source storage identities, physical bindings and title-specific tests resolve members; canonical aggregation remains LIVE_UNPROVEN if only part of a family is live.
- All audit entries are closed as findings/classifications or terminal evidence limits. Hypothetical PR merge vectors are UNKNOWN where conflicts remain or changed native/content admission and full proofs are unverified; E2E rows and questpc4b have exact zero classification deltas; that is a concrete evidence limit, not an unexamined row.

Corrections to the first published sweep: Golden was falsely included among the four live proof candidates because its test spawns King Tibianus, who has no shipped or admitted dynamic placement. The three former classic LIVE assignments (Dawnport, Troll Sabotage, Rottin Wood) also depended on absent fixture NPCs. Conversely, the first sweep inspected only 68 direct recipes: another 242 nested recipes exist. The final inventory incorporates all 310 recipes and actual physical bindings. qready2 claim shards are **not** identical to main; only its cited test/proof slice is already identical. Evidence: `npc-bindings.json` rows and `branch-assessments.json` qready2 record in the evidence directory.
