## Assessment

The suite contains substantial positive and adversarial coverage of atomic admission, immutable replay, generation fencing and reconnect continuity. Its test definitions do not establish runtime passage, deployment configuration or real Platform producer behavior. Source-controlled synthetic keys, direct administrative corruption, hand-authored publication evidence and process-local reopening are explicitly qualified above. No new independently qualified finding was established by this review.
