## Physical boundary

The existing `ChannelActorCarrier` retains one `CreatureOccupied` variant inside its already bounded slot array. At most one such creature is admitted to one carrier, even if other finite slots remain free. It has explicit fixture initial HP, an owner-bound target marker, exact actor-local generation, optional position and one optional committed result. The result contains complete canonical plan bytes (not a hash), including the occurrence bytes, damage, HP before and HP after. The result has no growing replay collection. The target marker occupies one bounded `Arc<[u8]>` of at most 4096 bytes allocated before admission. Slot overhead before commit is one bounded `Option<OwnerCommitRecord>`; after commit one `Box<[u8]>` retains at most 4096 complete encoded plan bytes, allocated before the single slot replacement. Existing finite carrier capacity and checked slot-size arithmetic remain unchanged; no production capacity claim is made.

Ability validates the typed `EffectPlan` against `ResolvedExactActor`: equal full occurrence/revisions, exactly one target/candidate/effect, matching client/AI source, atomic mode, positive damage and effect target in the plan's sole resolved target. Its canonical full-field encoding includes all fields retained by `EffectPlan`: occurrence, five revisions, proposal source, actor marker, counts, resolved target, effect kind and magnitude, stages, owner scope, group and mode. The original proposal candidate identities are not retained by `AbilityIntent`; only their count is retained and bound. The owner independently enforces actual encoded length <=4096, compares the effect target to its own slot's target marker, validates live continuity and slot-local generation at the write, computes checked HP transition, allocates one receipt, then changes HP and receipt together in that slot. The fixture `AbilityEngine` map does not participate.
