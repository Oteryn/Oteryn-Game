manual. Full consumable, leaderboard and prize semantics remain in M and A.

Accepted role constants (A4): each living Domestikion contributes **+10%** damage,
capped at **+50%**; Murmillion paralyzes every **10 s**, duration **5 s**;
Scissorion invisibility lasts **3 s** or until its next attack. The buff magnitude
and condition durations are decided Oteryn values. Q1's qualitative observations
do not provide exact native movement speed, every attack interval/chance,
Hoodinion's two tile masks or chain falloff, or Scissorion's critical odds/factor.

### Nine modifier policies

Percent HP damage uses **current** HP. Values are the accepted A4 policies,
cross-referenced to M; Chebyshev radius 1, deterministic draws and related
implementation choices must retain their Oteryn-decision provenance.

| Modifier | Trigger / timing | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Somersault | Each melee attack, **15%** | Move monster next to the farthest participant. |
| Going Down With Me | Monster death | **40%** current HP; last-hitter-centered area, radius **1**. |
| Exploding Corpses | Monster death | **25%** current HP; corpse-centered area, radius **1**. |
| That Escalated Quickly | Monster HP **<25%** of maximum | Damage uses **w+5**; no HP increase/heal. |
| The Floor is Lava | Every **15 s**; mark **100** tiles; resolve **3 s** later | Standing participant loses **60%** current HP. |
| Beam me Up! | Every **15 s**; **100** tiles; **3 s** warning | Teleport standing participant to a free walkable room tile. |
| Tanked Up | Every **15 s**; **100** tiles; **3 s** warning | Drunk for **10 s**, incoming damage **+10%** during it. |
| Sown Sorrow | Seed every **20/17/14/11/8 s** for group size 1/2/3/4/5 | Step within **6 s**; otherwise all participants fear **2 s**, incoming damage **+15%**. |
| Bad Roots | Same seed periods and **6 s** deadline | Otherwise all participants root **3 s**, incoming damage **+25%**. |

A4 freezes seed frequency to **admission group size**, even after a defeat. Tile
overlap resolves once per participant per pulse. R explicitly leaves overlapping
10/15/25% bonus algebra and cleansing/bonus lifetime undecided; the mechanics
fixture's behavior cannot approve those choices.

## Exact remaining capture and implementation checklist

1. **Close the five appearance bindings.** Use P's IDs with the existing
   `AppearanceIndex::outfit` / `resolve_outfit` renderer in
   `crates/client-assets/src/appearance_methods_1.rs`. Record client version,
   appearance/catalog/sheet hashes, direction and look type. Compare rendered
   identities to dated, named Global gameplay or a qualified structured source;
   retain name evidence separately from membership. No redistribution of the
   source sprite sheets is needed. The object-only
   `world-authoring/client_appearance_reader.py` does not read outfits.
2. **Capture profiles without guessing clocks.** For each species record source
   URL/revision, video timestamp or observation interval, client/world/version,
   wave, party count, active modifiers, target, position/direction, and every
   observed cast/step time. Separate isolated target casts from melee. Capture
   Hoodinion's two oriented ice masks as tile offsets, fire-chain target order,
   spacing and damage falloff; Scissorion poison normal/critical event counts and
   factor; each species' movement on identified ground and attack intervals.
   Preserve sample size/uncertainty. Animation duration does not prove speed;
   observed minimum intervals do not prove hidden probability or exact clocks.
3. **Recover the actual room.** Start from a verified Drome lobby/lever and record
   entry pads, lever identity/action, all entry/defeat/exit/return coordinates
   with floor convention, room bounds, walkable/blocked cells and spawn/mark
   constraints. Inspect generic donor maps as candidates, not only filenames.
   Tie any map extraction to exact donor commit/file hash and compare to named
   gameplay/accepted geometry. Lobby NPC coordinates alone fail this check.
4. **Resolve recorded policy decisions separately.** Obtain an explicit choice
   for A7 migration of historical epoch ordinals without corrupting scores,
   awards or receipts, and incoming-bonus overlap/cleansing. Keep any still
   unknown Global detail separate from an approved Oteryn default. A visual
   teleport effect remains optional/unknown unless specifically required.
5. **Populate through existing native authoring/admission.** Admit the five
   profiles, outfit bindings and room; replace the legacy spawn choice with the
   accepted `drome_wave` selection through existing deterministic RNG. Preserve
   existing HP/condition/death/reward owners. Source qualification must precede
   enabling `qualified_context()` for production.
6. **Verify the real caller after admission.** Exercise the actual public entry
   and lever/group handoff for counts 1–5, exact five-species membership, seeded
   replay, wave 0, below-best scoring, timeout, member departure, defeat, cap
   299→300, return/vitals/cooldown and rotation continuity. Exercise every
   modifier through canonical HP/condition/movement owners, including last
   hitter/familiar center and receipt replay. Existing focused filters are
   listed in R. Passing mechanics fixtures alone does not close W17.
