## Result and evidence limits

The crystal/cannon loop is recoverable from multiple sources. Full public
admission is **not source-qualified**: exact lever/staging/return anchors,
the executable per-character quest predicate, several actor schedules and
the vulnerability duration remain missing or conflicting. Keep public entry
disabled until those data and their production consumers are qualified. A
second isolated test-only phase machine would not close this gap.

Video transcripts below were read in the public YouTube interface. They are
**author reports**, classified as `COMMUNITY_CORROBORATION`, not measured
gameplay observations. The player remained buffering, so no frame timing,
map-coordinate extraction or direct combat measurement is claimed. The
connected local-browser plugin was unavailable; public transcript reading
used the cloud browser. Official news text was available in indexed search
results; direct page retrieval returned HTTP 403 and was not retried.

## Proposed production composition

This section is a **RECOMMENDATION**, not a second encounter authority.

1. **Admission.** Extend the existing shared boss activity catalogue only when
   its exact map objects, party staging tiles, room template and per-character
   quest eligibility are resolved. Revalidate the complete roster, current
   sessions, selected difficulty and retry state in the existing admission
   transaction. The reported level recommendations in videos are not minimum
   level gates. Do not infer admission from the recipe's `s9` collect stage.
2. **Bind one encounter.** Bind exact actor incarnations, admitted sessions,
   room scope generation and content pins through existing owners. Start
   encounter-local cannon/portal/soul state afresh as a design recommendation.
   C2 requires correct starting charges but does not publish their numeric
   initial count or establish how the corrected bug arose.
3. **Protected cycle.** Use existing map/spawn, damage, item-instance and
   inventory owners. Consume one valid crystal atomically with its selected
   cannon's charge increment; duplicate or failed uses must not charge a
   cannon or remove another item. Charge state and crystal lifetime belong
   to the encounter/item lifecycle, not durable Quest progress.
4. **Release and reset.** Both full cannons trigger one protected-to-vulnerable
   transition. The verified duration must come from qualified source data;
   do not choose thirty or forty-five by convenience. On expiry, return the
   same living boss to its protected anchor and begin a fresh loading cycle.
   Preserve HP and use the actual source rules for charge reset and active adds.
5. **Concurrent hazards.** Existing combat and condition owners handle soul
   agony, holy-only damage eligibility, tank interactions and custodian
   movement. Do not copy O1's precise schedules, healing coefficients or cap.
   Higher-difficulty modifiers require their own qualified field-level data.
6. **Terminal settlement.** An exact native boss death reaches the existing
   participant-aware quest/reward writers once. Timeout, evacuation, disconnect
   and instance retirement cancel owned timers and remove transient encounter
   effects. Include room-exit visual cleanup as a regression invariant (C5);
   complete condition cancellation is the proposed lifecycle design, not a
   claim made by that patch. Never award whole-quest completion for the first
   Mimar kill.

enter through the production admission and event path. A parameterized reducer
or a passing fixture with fabricated anchors does not prove W16 closure.
