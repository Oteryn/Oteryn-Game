## Outcome

Section 12.5 step 1: the vocabulary. Section 12 is ACCEPTED, with the owner answers recorded in 12.1-12.4 and 12.6.

| Extension | Schema (`build_schema.py`, regenerated) | Semantic check (`validate_encounter.py`) |
|---|---|---|
| CW2-1 | `teleport.who` and the `in_anchor` subject take `{triggering: true}`; the shared action `subject` does not | only in a trigger fired by one creature or in `area_entered`/`area_left`; `remove triggering` also fails with `who: player` |
| CW2-2 | `stepped_on` takes exactly one of `item` and `corpse_of` | `corpse_of` names a known role |
| CW2-3 | `map_item` takes exactly one of `item` and `triggering` | `triggering` only with `remove`, only in `stepped_on`, and with no `into`, place, destination, revert, effect or interaction |
| CW2-4 | `random_in` takes an optional boolean `free` | unchanged: the area check still applies |

No trigger, condition or action kind is added. The E1 counts (17, 14, 25) are unchanged.

Owner answers applied in section 12:
- Q4 Alptramun: follow global. Existing vocabulary covers it with no new term: one `creature_died` rule per tier with
  a `spawn` at `death_position`, capped at tier 4. The dreams' healing stays Canary monster data, and the initial
  dreams are the lever's two.
- Q5 Gorzindel: the return uses `in_anchor` of `triggering`, for players still in the instance.
- Q6 Sandking: the broods use `random_in free: true`.
- Q8 Melting Frozen Horror: a separate unconditional rule removes the Solid form. This deviates from Canary.
