## Outcome

- `ability/creature_bite.rs`: `AiAbilityAdapter::bite` builds a typed `AiBiteIntent` (issuer and
  target are `ExactActorRef`, occurrence = issuer + think sequence). `commit_ai_bite` is the
  Channel-owner work item: stale issuer rejected first; then the target is the committed player
  of its GameSession, range 1 on one floor under one position context, not re-entry protected,
  the bite interval elapsed; the damage is one `EffectPlan` with `ProposalSource::Ai`; the
  vitals owner applies it floored at 1 and the cooldown is written in the same step. The first
  result of an occurrence (accepted or rejected) is kept; a retry returns it, an older occurrence
  is `OccurrenceSuperseded`, another target is `OccurrenceConflict`. Nothing is buffered.
- `CreatureBiteLedger` keeps one entry per live creature, bounded by the D57 derived bound (64 =
  16 x 4, §4.9); dead issuers are pruned before a new entry, `retire` drops one at once.
- Carrier: `ChannelRuntimeV1::contains_live_creature` and `read_actor_position` (read-only), and
  test-only creature admission and removal.
