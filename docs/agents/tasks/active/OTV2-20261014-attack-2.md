## Outcome

- **Weapon melee (ATTACK-0 §4):** a swing draws with `player_melee_formula` and the held
  weapon's attack when a hand holds a club, sword, axe or fist weapon. Otherwise it uses fists.
  - The hands are read left (6) then right (5), as `player.cpp:405-464` `getWeapon` does.
    Shields, ammunition and spellbooks are skipped. A distance weapon or a wand counts as no
    weapon.
  - The revision is `formula:player-melee` for a weapon and `formula:player-fist` for fists.
  - The weapon is read from the equipment owner under the current item fence and the Channel
    content pin, by the attacker's own connection, at most once per second while it holds a
    target. The read reuses `observe_character_equipment`'s path through
    `read_current_equipment`.
  - A swing waits while the read is missing, was made under another pin, or is older than one
    attack interval.
  - An equipment move forgets the read and bumps an epoch, so a read that raced the move is
    not stored.
  - A hand item with an unknown policy, or with weapon facts that are not Known, gives no
    weapon: the swing fails closed.
- **Creature block, defence and armor (ATTACK-0 §5, `creature.cpp:958-990`):**
  - Each live creature a player hits gets a block budget, created at the first hit and pruned
    when the creature is no longer live. The ledger is bounded by the actor slot capacity.
  - A blocked hit meets the creature's content defence, `uniform[defense / 2, defense]`,
    which is Canary's raw `Monster::getDefense` (`monster.cpp:242-251`). When the defence stops
    the whole hit, armor is skipped.
  - Every hit meets armor through `armor_reduction_bounds`.
  - The draws are `defence-draw` / `attack.defence` and `armor-draw` / `attack.armor`.
  - A creature policy without defence or armor takes no hit (fail closed).
  - `CompiledCreaturePolicy` gains `defense`, compiled from the creature content
    `details.defense`.
- **VIS negative floors (VIS-1):** a source world's native floor sees as the Tibia floor of its
  negation (`tibia_floor`). Thalom's -8..-5 floors now have the reference view.
  - The attack, AI targeting and wake-box `PLANE`/`VIEW_FLOORS` workarounds are gone.
  - `VisibilityPosition` admits -15..=15.

## Excluded

- Elemental weapon damage. Live skills: skills stay at `starting_skill`.
- Player defence and shield against creature melee.
- Canary's forge defence multiplier, and the scaled `DefenceSource::Creature` formula. The raw
  content defence is used instead; this is a review point.
- Distance weapons, throwing weapons and wands (RANGED). PvP.
