## Outcome

The active generation's Item provider gains canonical profiles for the closed coin table of
`apps/game-server/src/domain/currency.rs` (CHARACTER-GOLD-FEE-BOUNDARY-V1, D175/D176): gold
`i3031`, platinum `i3035`, crystal `i3043`. Each is materializable, `StackCapable` with
`stack_max` 100 (`COIN_STACK_MAXIMUM`) and legal in `CharacterInventory` and `Ground`, so a
reward row dropping coins settles and the coin can be picked up. The coin table, worths and
stack maximum are unchanged.

- `build_source_item_profiles.py` adds the three coin ids to its source set, refuses a coin
  record that disagrees with D176 and writes the coin overlay `coin-item-profiles.json` beside
  the full provider (121 records).
- The pinned r25 pack is not edited. `register_spell_families.py` merges the committed overlay
  into the Item provider after the compose stage, checks the closed coin key set, the D176 facts
  and that the result equals the generator's full provider digest, and repins `item_profiles`.
- Generated by the tools: `spell-native-profiles.json`, `spells.manifest.json`, the Item index,
  `content/manifest.json` and `content.lock.json`.
- No migration, wire, persistence, `.github` or `Cargo` change. Loot tables and Item keys
  already carry the coins as materializable.
