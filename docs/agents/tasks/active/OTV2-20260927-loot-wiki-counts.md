## Outcome

The owner asked (2026-09-27) to use the wiki to confirm the loot of monsters and bosses. D32 extends the
D15 loot rules to counts:
- **Counts outside the Canary range.** Where a Canary entry adopts the wiki probability (at least 10
  drops), a Loot Statistics count outside the Canary min–max replaces that range. Under this rule, 740
  counts of 325 fully resolved monsters take the wiki range.
- **Canary `minCount` 0.** Such an entry becomes count 1..max. With wiki statistics, the wiki probability
  is kept. Without them, the probability is scaled by max/(max+1), which is exactly the engine
  behaviour. This admits Duke Krule, so wave A has 1,316 monsters.

Authority: direct owner request in this session. Runtime behaviour stays unallocated.
