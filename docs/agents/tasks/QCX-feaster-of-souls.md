## Blockers and open facts

Crystal paths below are relative to `data-global/scripts/` at the pinned revision. Main already has generic boss room, cooldown and quest death infrastructure. The ordinary quest movement callback requires personal state for `minimum_level`, and the bounded single-player STEP encounter entry accepts only encounter effects without timing. These restrictions apply at the Crystal entrance and solo-room triggers respectively.
