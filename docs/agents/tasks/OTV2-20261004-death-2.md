## Outcome

- **Trigger (§4.1):** D54's floor at 1 is retired. `creature_damage` takes health to 0 and never
  below; a hit to 0 is lethal. The D54 names `FlooredDamage`/`floor_creature_damage` remain only
  as aliases that `spell::cast` still imports (CP: no other cast.rs line changes).
- **Occurrence (§4.2):** the vitals owner (`ChannelSpellStates`) mints one UUIDv7
  `PlayerDeathOccurrence` at the lethal write and records the death with its cell in the same
  vitals revision; the bite's kept result carries it, so a retry of the lethal think occurrence
  returns it and never kills twice. A failed mint or position read refuses the hit with nothing
  written. A dead player takes no step, `USE`, cast, damage, credit or Serene change until its
  respawn.
- **Durable death (§4.3, DEATH-0 §3.4-§3.5):** `DurabilityRoot::settle_player_death` commits the
  death in the Character's revision slot (a retained receipt replays at its original revision; a
  missing progression row runs the D88 initializer first; an earlier death's unconsumed respawn is
  consumed first), then consumes its own pending respawn. `consume_pending_respawn` deletes the
  row under the recovery fence, the admission relation locks and the full gameplay fence; it
  advances no revision and a retry deletes nothing twice. No migration: 0016 already grants the
  runtime DELETE.
- **Respawn (§4.5, D63):** on the 1000 ms vitals cadence the Channel owner settles the death off
  its lane (no lock across the database), then in one owner step places the actor at its respawn
  position (`place_respawned_player`, one position successor) and refills health and mana under
  one new `ACTOR_VITALS` revision (`PlayerSpellState::respawned`), which the cadence publishes as
  its delta. A durable attempt without an outcome keeps the player dead and the next tick retries
  the same occurrence.
- **Restart (DEATH-0 §3.4):** a fresh admission places its new actor at the entry spawn with full
  vitals and consumes the Character's pending respawn, in the revision slot, before the quest
  admission writes.

## Rulings and stated assumptions (reversible)

- **CP ruling:** until a temple/town decision exists, the respawn position is the character's
  admitted entry/bootstrap spawn: the pinned Content generation's first-entry start cell. The
  actor keeps its reference (teleport, full HP and mana, a new vitals revision), so
  `connection.rs` is untouched.
- **CP ruling (option A):** owned paths extended with `actor_spell.rs`,
  `channel_owner_creature_bite_tests.rs`, `durability/character_death.rs` and the death writer
  PostgreSQL cases; `spell/cast.rs` gets only `PlayerSpellState::respawned`, in its own impl block
  at the end.
- Durable cells are the big-endian `(x, y, floor)` bytes; the death cell's map revision is
  `map:` plus the pinned map revision digest in hex.
- No path inserts blessings before DEATH-4, so the death intent's held set is empty.
- No Character progression policy is composed into the gameplay seam yet
  (`player_death_progression()` is `None`, like `character_cast_facts`), so a runtime death
  respawns without a durable death until that owner supplies the policy. Production actors have no
  vitals yet either (`character_cast_facts` is `None`), and no production path issues creature
  bites (`commit_ai_bite` is test-only), so the trigger is proven at owner level.
- Respawn position emission to the client is lane E (client observation of death and respawn);
  this lane publishes the respawn vitals through the existing cadence only.
- An occupied respawn tile is not checked, as first entry does not check it.
