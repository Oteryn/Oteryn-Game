## Split

The decision's COND-1 row does not fit one reviewable batch (about 500 hand-written lines), so it
is split as CHAR-BUILD-1 was:

- **COND-1a (this PR):** the pure per-actor store in `ability/condition.rs`: the six families and
  13 conflict keys (§3), the per-family policies (§3, §3.1), provenance frozen at application
  (§3.2), the named draws and the tick order with `RUN_EACH_BOUNDED` overload (§3.4), the
  admission refusals (content immunity, PvE re-entry, `COND0-RL-01`) and death (§6.1).
- **COND-1b (next, pure, `ability/condition.rs` only):** the §3.3 mana shield split, §5 dispel
  and Cleanse with its immunity, and the §6.2 channel handover and resume. This is where the
  three CONDITIONS-0 MEDIUMs land: Cleanse determinism (candidates in instance-sequence order,
  drowning excluded, no draw for zero or one, one `COND_CLEANSE_PICK` draw for two or more);
  Cleanse immunity crossing reconnect and transfer with its remaining time; the speed charms
  staying failing closed (`charm_effects.rs` is untouched until SPEED-1 and the AI cadence
  amendment). The code is drafted and tested; it is held back only for batch size.
- **COND-1c (then):** owner wiring. The runtime actor holds a store; each `ConditionTick` becomes
  one Effect Plan committed like any damage (resistances, the mana shield stage, death); the
  source is looked up at each tick and an absent source deals the tick with no attacker and no
  damage-record entry; a tick refreshes the in-fight deadline (ATTACK-0); offensive charms do not
  fire on ticks; the transfer continuation carries the store. It touches `foundation/**` and the
  vitals owner, so it needs its own allocation of those paths.

## Outcome

- Values come only from `ConditionDefinition` (content); code owns families and policies. A
  definition with a tick interval under `COND0-RL-02` (1,000 ms), a zero amount or duration, or an
  inverted damage range is refused.
- `SPEED`: haste and paralysis replace each other; the target speed is drawn with
  `COND_SPEED_DRAW` in thousandths, truncated once; paralysis floors at 40 and leaves a base under
  40 unchanged; the delta is fixed at application.
- `DAMAGE_OVER_TIME`: replaces only when the new midpoint is strictly greater than the remaining
  total, a field always replaces; a replacement keeps the tick timing and takes the new
  provenance; a non-`delayed` definition deals one tick at once (Canary parity, new and
  replacement alike); a same-element field does not use ticks up; a protection zone refuses and
  uses them up. The total is drawn with `COND_DOT_TOTAL_DRAW`.
- `FOOD_REGENERATION`: adds time, `Full` when remaining plus added reaches 1,200 s; ticks in a
  protection zone are suppressed while the time runs down. `RECOVERY` refreshes and keeps its
  tick timing; `MANA_SHIELD` refreshes and resets its capacity; `LIGHT` replaces on a duration at
  least the remaining one and decays linearly.
- Ticks come out of `take_due` in `(due, sequence)` order, at most `COND0-RL-03` (4) damage ticks
  per actor per pass, the rest in the next pass, none dropped; `channel_tick_order` merges actors
  by `(due, actor, sequence)`. `COND0-RL-01` (16) refuses a new key past it.
- PvE re-entry (§6.1): a creature source is refused on a protected target, and a protected
  player's own application on a creature is refused; fields and PvP are unaffected.
