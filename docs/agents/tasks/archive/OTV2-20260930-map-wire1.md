## Outcome

MAP-WIRE-1 is the contract candidate for map state on the wire (#162 5914289140; ADR-0021 §5).

- **Design (M1 b, Tibia parity):** the server describes the tiles of the interest area from the
  bundle and the channel overlay; the client holds no map file.
- **Allowlist:** appearance, count or subtype where needed, stack position, and `placement_key` only
  for actionable items; production bundles only.
- **Consistency:** a map-item pickup publishes its hide and its domain 1 entity in one sync unit
  after the MINT commits; one order on a tile across both domains; Tibia's 10-thing display cap.
- **Handles:** `MapItemRef` for command 9 and use.
- **Stream:** per-floor snapshots and `AREA_ENTER` / `TILE_SET` deltas, with snapshot triggers;
  bounds at the largest area; measurement before activation; capability `MAP_STATE_V1`.

No code, migration or content change is made. Owner acceptance is required (M1).
