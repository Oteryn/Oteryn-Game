## Outcome

- Migration 0076 adds `game_character_inbox_cause_kinds`, `game_character_inbox_counters`,
  `game_item_character_inbox_locations` (Character + World, no ChannelId) and
  `game_character_inbox_deliveries`. Inbox rows and delivery records are immutable and cannot be
  truncated.
- `game_character_inbox_deliver` is SECURITY DEFINER and not executable by the runtime role; a
  caller (HOUSE-1b, MARKET-1) reaches it from its own SECURITY DEFINER function and audits the
  returned `(character_id, ordinal)`. No session fence, no capacity refusal (MARKET0-RL-06 is
  MARKET-1's). Lock order: root FOR KEY SHARE, item, counter FOR UPDATE.
- Typed refusals OTI01 (contents), OTI02 (World), OTI03 (still in another location at commit),
  OTI04 (unknown cause kind, before any foreign key: #1780 finding 4178336131) and OTI05
  (duplicate delivery), classified by `CharacterInboxDeliveryError`.
- `game_item_location_exclusive` and `game_item_transfer_consistency_guard` count the Inbox
  location; their bodies are the union with every earlier migration's body.
- Read helpers in `durability::character_inbox`; no runtime writer.
- Compatibility: additive tables and functions; the two replaced guards keep every earlier check.
  No wire, protocol, identity or contract change.
