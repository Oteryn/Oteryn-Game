## Outcome

PREM-1 was split for size: this PR is **PREM-1a**.

- Migration 0029: the durable consumer fence. It has an immutable evidence log keyed by
  (account, `authority_revision`) with semantic fingerprints, which is the equivocation horizon;
  an account high water with a sticky conflict marker; and an (account, entitlement) lifecycle
  high water. Triggers only let the fence advance.
- `durability::premium_fence`: fences one snapshot per transaction. The outcome is accepted,
  replayed, stale or conflict.
- `premium::snapshot`: validates `oteryn.premium_snapshot.v1`, including the nonce and account
  binding, the compatibility record and the lease bound.
- `premium::PremiumConsumer`:
  - classifies per consumer §8;
  - applies `premium-surfaces-1` (`REQUIRE_CURRENT` everywhere);
  - provides `premium_current` and `premium_entitlement_ended`;
  - re-proves authority after a restart before any benefit.

**PREM-1b**, which needs a fresh allocation, covers the mTLS snapshot client, nonce generation,
refresh scheduling and the in-process test producer. It is blocked on the request side of
PREMIUM-DELIVERY-0 §3 (the escalation on #162).

## High-risk authority/recovery qualification

`AuthorityInvariant × ConsumerBoundary × MutationOperator` for the one boundary, fence then
authorize:

- **Identity and binding.** Tested: another account, another nonce, and another entitlement
  (which opens its own row). `NONE` requires a null entitlement.
- **Liveness and authority.**
  - Tested: stale revision; delayed older ACTIVE after REVOKE or NONE; replay; equivocation at
    the current and at a historical revision; lifecycle regression; restart before re-proof;
    quarantine on a fence write failure (unit).
  - Concurrent first insert: fails closed through a unique violation.
- **Temporal and provenance.**
  - Tested: conservative start and end; lease cutoff; the commercial end before the lease;
    unsynchronized time; lease above policy; unknown schema, product, version or profile.
  - Build provenance and `refresh_after` are excluded from the fingerprint.
- **Durable guards.** Tested: lowering, conflict clearing, delete and truncate are rejected, and
  the runtime-role grants are checked.
