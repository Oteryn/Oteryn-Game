## Outcome

- **Wire (`protocol-oteryn::world_map`, `world_map_v1.proto`).** The domain 17 snapshot and delta
  codecs, the 40-byte `WorldObjectTargetV1`, capability 18 (requires 4 and 6, `offered: false`)
  and domain 17 in the registry, `MAPW-RL-01..04` in the limits registry, and the handle bound
  `ITEMV0-RL-03-MAP-VIEW` (1325; 1661 with capability 14). Capability 18 is added to
  `REGISTERED_CAPABILITY_IDS_V1`.
- **Composition (`map/view.rs`).** Base plus overlay stacks, the 10-entry cut, the visible-floor
  resolver and the budget order. Per-entry facts come from an injected `MapFacts` resolver
  (option c, control plane D710); `WorldBase` is unchanged.
- **Session view (`gameplay_transport/world_map.rs`).** Plans the whole window on each update and
  diffs full wire tiles against the tiles last sent, so every budget membership change resends
  each affected tile (deferred Codex P2 4179523799). Over `MAPW-RL-03`, a floor change, a jump or
  an identity change sends a snapshot. Map handles are a view of the session handle table,
  replaced clone-then-commit.
- **Connection hook.** With capability 18 the join adds the domain 17 snapshot (single chunk) and
  a step or visibility tick sends a delta; an unserved map, an error or a mid-session snapshot ends
  the session, and the client resumes. The fixture World serves no map, so capability 18 fails
  closed until MAP-CUTOVER-1 composes a resolver.
- **Command 9** from a base-entry handle is `ITEM_MOVE_OUTCOME_NOT_SUPPORTED`
  (`move_source_outcome`) until MAP-PICKUP-1.

## Measurement

`MAP01-VIEWPORT-US`, release build, 18x14 over floors -7..=0 with every third tile holding three
more items, 2,000 join snapshots (composition plus encode, server side only):
p50 2.04 ms, **p99 3.47 ms**, max 8.24 ms. This breaches the 100 us budget and blocks the
capability 18 offer gate. In one split, planning took 1.09 ms, the handle table and tiles 0.74 ms,
and encoding 0.43 ms.
