## Result

- The map loader keeps the bundle's validated spawn family (`map::load_spawns`); `check` stores it on the checked bundle and the booted `BundleWorld` (format v3 spawn frame, already decoded and validated by `oteryn-world-bundle`).
- `table_activation_spawns` turns the family into the spawn-source seam SPAWN-1a built: per source, the points that share a creature and a respawn delay form one group in authored order, cell `(x, y, -floor)`, occupancy retry 5,000 ms. Points of a creature whose period is not `All` are outside the activation set.
- A bundle World now realizes those groups at Channel activation, before the listener binds (`realize_bundle_spawns`). The initial health of a creature is its served definition's maximum health; a creature the active content does not serve refuses readiness (`creature spawn realization`). The fixture World keeps the D116 source.
- Merged with main after SPAWN-CEILINGS-1 (#1984). The carrier's fixture-era 16 sources / 4 cells are gone (Codex P1 4241672767): activation is checked against `CREATUREAI0-RL-01` to `-03` and the carrier's player headroom.
- `CREATUREAI0-RL-14` (Codex P1 4241840141): boot begins the activation once (`begin_activation_spawns`, every ceiling checked, nothing admitted) and realizes it with `realize_activation_spawn_window`, at most 4,096 points per 50 ms owner window, on a boot-local monotonic clock that waits for the next window boundary between windows (`realize_activation_paced`). A shutdown requested meanwhile stops boot before readiness. The boot event reports `windows=N`. Tested: 8,253 points realize in 3 windows over at least 100 ms; a cancelled boot stops after the first window.

## Blocker before merge (out of owned paths)

- The carrier holds one creature and one respawn delay per spawn source, so this task splits each bundle source into runs of points sharing both. On the pinned pre-production bundle (`5c32d7ac...`: 51,078 sources, 82,673 active points) that gives 70,206 carrier sources, above `CREATUREAI0-RL-02` (65,536) and the carrier's `u16` source ordinal: `begin_activation_spawns` refuses `CapacityExceeded` and the bundle World refuses readiness. 9,468 bundle sources mix creatures or delays. CREATURE-AI-0 §6.1 counts RL-02 by bundle source and gives each point its own creature and delay.
- The fix is in `apps/game-server/src/foundation/runtime_actor_carrier.rs` (not owned here): one carrier source per bundle source, with per-point creature, delay and initial health in `ActivationSpawnFacts` / `SpawnDefinition`. Grouping by distinct (creature, delay) per source instead would give 64,472 sources, 1.6 % under RL-02, and would break the canonical (source key, point ordinal) order, so it is not used.

## Excluded scope and follow-ups

- `CREATUREAI0-RL-15` and `-16` are not measured here; they need the reference map.
- Night-period points wait for a day/night source.
