## Outcome

EQUIP-0 makes worn equipment work at Tibia parity, item 2 of the architect's base-mechanics
close-out plan (#162 5929069698), widened from TIMED-ITEM-0.

- **Abilities:** a closed typed set (skill and stat boosts, speed, protections, regeneration, mana
  shield, condition suppression, light), active only in the item's own slot with its requirements;
  one deterministic Reference evaluation plan; protections summed with imbuement absorbs
  (`PARITY_PENDING`).
- **Time:** equip and unequip forms as `PRESERVE_INSTANCE` transforms; `WHILE_EQUIPPED` and
  `WHILE_HELD` budgets on IMBUE-FORGE-0's 60 s checkpoint model; expiry to `expire_to` or a burn;
  torches lit by an ITEM-USE-0 `Transform`; soft boots repaired at an NPC for a fee.
- **Charges:** spent from a durable reserve of 4, so a crash never returns a charge.
- **DUR-03:** the closed `ItemLifeCause` and `ItemUseCause::Transform`, the server-originated
  composition variant, numeric rows.
- **Owner questions:** none; R1-R4 are architect rulings.

No code, migration or content change is made.
