## Outcome

- **Swing timer and drain:** the Channel owner's cycle runs the swing timer. Each due swing
  drains as `AutoAttack` through GAME-ABILITY-01, using the `commit_exact_owner_swing_damage`
  bridge.
- **Creature melee:** creatures melee their AI target. Charm hooks run against an empty
  `CharmStateRead` (decision 3A).
- **Swing identity:** each swing is identified by `(character, lease_gen, session, lineage
  CommandRef of ATTACK_TARGET, swing_ordinal u16)`, and the commit is deferred.
  - A retained receipt replays `Ok` with `applied = false`.
  - A lower or equal unretained ordinal returns `StaleAttackerSequence`.
  - A new target starts a new lineage at ordinal 0.
  - On u16 overflow, the target is cleared.
  - The high-water mark is never lowered.
- **Commands 11 and 12:** dispatched with 25/s windows.
- **Domain 10:** emitted.
- **Capability 17:** offered; it requires 6.
- **RL-03:** the in-fight row is 60 s.
- **Logout and closed client:** `logout_blocked = in_fight_until.is_some()` (decision 4A). A
  closed client holds the character while it is in fight.
- **Creature death:** the live drain projects the lethal hit through
  `project_fixed_one_creature_death` (decision 1A). A Postgres case shows swing-committed damage
  composing with `settle_creature_death_rewards` when the settlement is called explicitly.
- **Acceptance amendment (CP D655):** §2.3 is amended so that live loot and XP settlement of an
  auto-attack kill belongs to KILL-REWARD-COMP-1; this task only projects the death.
- **Closed client in fight:** a disconnected actor held by its in-fight deadline stays a creature
  melee target with no re-entry window, so a monster hit extends the hold. The melee pass reads
  a census that keeps control-lost players, and the hold itself runs that pass at least once per
  think interval, so a held actor that is the Channel's last connection still takes hits.
  The grace wait runs in the same hold steps while the actor is in fight. A held actor that dies
  is no longer held, and both release paths settle its recorded death before the actor leaves.
