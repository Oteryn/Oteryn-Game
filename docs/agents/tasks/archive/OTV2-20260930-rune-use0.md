## Outcome

RUNE-USE-0 designs rune use and rune conjuring for full Global parity (owner direction
2026-09-30), and settles the rune deferral of ITEM-USE-0.

- **Item.** A rune is a stack of 100; one use burns one unit. `rune.charges` is a legacy value
  equal to the conjure count.
- **Wire.** Capability `RUNE_USE_V1` (number at allocation): a position arm for USE field 4 and
  dispositions `TARGET_ILLEGAL` and `PROTECTION_ZONE`; conjure adds `REAGENT_MISSING` and
  `NO_ROOM` to the spell cast result.
- **Value.** Rune use is a one-unit BURN under `ItemUseCause::Rune` (not `DECAY_RETIRE`);
  conjure is one reagent BURN plus one MINT under the closed `ConjureCause`. Both commit before
  the effect.
- **Cast.** Every check at PREPARE, then a frozen cast with pending cooldowns and holds; the
  frozen plan applies through the ability pipeline on the known commit, so SPELL-D3 is kept. A
  separate rune slot per actor.
- **Fields.** Runtime, scope-ephemeral world objects with provenance, decay and a stable runtime
  identity, applying CONDITIONS-0 on step to creatures. A field rune needs
  `WORLD_SPATIAL_FIELDS`; fields that affect or block players (Magic Wall, Wild Growth,
  creature-made fields) wait for an FND-04 gameplay-admission rule (`RUNEUSE0-C7`, ruling R5).
- **Catalogue.** 36 rune carriers: 18 direct, 1 condition, 9 fields, 2 blocking, 6 native.
- **Architect ruling R1:** a rune whose target dies in the commit time is spent (owner rule
  5905825574; no owner question).

No code, migration or content change is made.
