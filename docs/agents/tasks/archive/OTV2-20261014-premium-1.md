## Result

- `domain::premium::PremiumGate`: the one value a Premium-gated decision reads. It is closed by
  `Default` and `PremiumGate::CLOSED`, and only a Premium fence owner opens it
  (`pub(crate) from_fence_read`). `require()` refuses with `PremiumRefusal::NotCurrent`;
  `premium_current()` feeds the existing soul and promotion rules.
- `SpellPremiumCoordinator::premium_gate(account)` and `RegisteredSpellAccessOwner::premium_gate()`:
  the account-level read of the same committed view the spell casts read. A Premium view now keeps
  its committed fence read (snapshot, conflict, fresh). The gate opens only when that read
  classifies as `Current` under the view's live clock epoch. The match is exhaustive: Free,
  Revoked, Expired, NotYetEffective, Unavailable and Conflicting all close it. No view, a denied
  or revoked epoch, a lost clock and a restart also close it.
- `durability/premium_fence.rs`: unchanged; no change needed.
- High-risk authority/recovery qualification: read-only authority gate. No durable write, no
  migration, no fencing, no wire change. The gate only narrows (fail closed); it never grants.
