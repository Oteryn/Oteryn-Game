## Outcome

The two P2 findings deferred from #1742 under D245:

1. Review thread 4177060427. A fresh admission reads the Character's pending respawn under the
   admitted session's fences, deleting nothing (`DurabilityRoot::pending_respawn`). It then places
   the new actor at the recorded respawn position as one position successor. The cell must be a
   Walkable cell of the pinned generation's movement cells and not a closed door. Only after the
   placement succeeds does it consume that exact occurrence. An undecodable or non-Walkable cell, a
   failed placement or a failed write leaves the obligation for the next admission.
2. Review thread 4177060433. `PlayerSpellState::respawned(now)` resets a monk's Harmony and forced
   Serene to 0, as `commit_character_death` writes them, and runs the Serene initialization
   evaluation at `now`. A monk that was detached while dead is therefore playable after the respawn.
