## Outcome

MAIL-0 starts the mail system on the owner's direction (2026-09-30: build now, full Tibia Global
parity).

- **Items.** Parcel, letter, label and their stamped forms; a stamp rule (`PRESERVE_INSTANCE`);
  base-map mailboxes as `container_fixture` bindings.
- **Text.** MAIL-0 owns durable writable text for letters and labels only: a text store keyed by
  the item, a fenced write, never logged or audited.
- **Posting.** Command 9 to a `MAILBOX` destination; the recipient is the first text line, resolved
  through `0022` in the sender's World; the town is ignored (one Inbox, 2b). Instant delivery in
  one transaction into the recipient's `CharacterInbox`, with the stamp. Misaddressed items stay in
  the backpack.
- **Parcels.** A root TRANSFER with at most 10 children without contents, after BAGS-0.
- **Anti-abuse.** 1 posting per second; a 50,000 Inbox mail ceiling so mail cannot lock a Market.
- **System letters** (owner Q1a): the HOUSE-OWN-0 rent warning as a minted stamped letter.
- **Juniors** (owner Q2b): letters only, no parcels, to or from a junior.

Pointer amendments, each pending on acceptance of MAIL-0: DUR-03 §38, MARKET-0 §5, HOUSE-OWN-0
§5, CHAT-0 §5, the composition decision. No code, migration or content change is made.
