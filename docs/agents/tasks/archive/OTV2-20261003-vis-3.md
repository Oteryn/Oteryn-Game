## Outcome

- **Channel entities.** `ChannelRuntimeV1::visible_entities` reads, without changing anything, the
  committed positioned players and live creatures (`health > 0`) under the pinned Movement context.
  It visits only the carrier's occupied-slot index (reserved at bootstrap, kept by admission,
  `remove`, the spawn rollbacks and the companion reserve, install and rollback paths), never the
  whole capacity (Codex 4178196302, 4178855592: installed companions are in the census).
  The production authority maps them to `ChannelEntities` by their placement identity.
- **Composition.** `SessionVisibility` (`world_spatial.rs`) indexes the Channel's entities in the
  VIS-1 `InterestIndex`, queries from the observer at the reference 18 × 14 area (own actor first,
  then canonical order, at most 256: the degrade disposition) and diffs each refresh against what the
  session was last sent. A diff of at most 256 entries is a type 2 delta; a larger one is the
  resync disposition: the connection ends with its continuity at the last delivered sequence and
  revision, and the resume of that continuity sends the domain 1 snapshot from the interest set
  (Codex 4178053966: an admitted `oteryn-session` client accepts no `SnapshotBegin` after its join).
- **Connection.** With capability 6 the join, resync and resume snapshot of domain 1 is composed
  from the interest set (after the item view, so objects carry their handles under capability 4).
  An own step sends the interest-set delta (the step's committed position is the observer's); a 250 ms refresh tick sends changes made by others.
  A Channel that cannot be read fails the session closed. Without capability 6 domain 1 stays the
  v1 own-actor type, unchanged.
- **Offering.** Capability 6 is `offered: true` in the registry and in
  `PRODUCTION_OFFERED_CAPABILITIES`; the production-path admission test selects it.
- **Known gaps.** Placeholders (D524): the runtime carries no facing, appearance or maximum health
  yet, so every actor is sent facing south, with `appearance_ref` 0 and health 100 %. Corpses
  (D525): not sent in domain 1 until the D3-7 corpse item binding (`i00005801`) lands, since no bound
  item definition reference exists for the wire; the corpse part of the acceptance is deferred to
  that binding.
- Not in scope: line of sight, invisibility, spectators, NPC actors, the D222 order (ITEM-MOVE-2b).
