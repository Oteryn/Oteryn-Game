## Outcome

- **Customer queue (§7):** each conversation records when its customer joined the NPC's queue.
  `ChatRuntime::newest_customer(npc)` is the tail, the customer the NPC faces; GAME-AI reads it
  for the turn (NPC-ACTOR-1). A topic in an open conversation keeps the customer's place; a
  customer who leaves and greets again joins at the tail. The queue stays bounded by
  `NPC0-RL-05` and is cleared with the conversations on admission, reconnect and transfer.
- **Range check on every committed step (§7, `NPC0-RL-07`):** a committed player step checks the
  stepping session's conversation. A customer beyond 4 tiles (Chebyshev, same floor) of its NPC,
  or whose NPC is gone, has its conversation closed at once. The NPC's walk-away line (the
  generated farewell; no NPC with an admitted Dialogue has a book entry yet) waits for the
  session's next domain 7 payload, which carries it to the speaker alone (Canary sends it
  privately). A pending line is sent as its own closed payload, even when the conversation was
  never shown open or another opened since (Codex P2 on #2123); the new one follows on the next
  tick. Every walk-away line pending since the last payload is kept, oldest first (Codex P2 on
  #2123).
- The same check runs when a relocating spell installs (the caster is the only relocated actor)
  and when an in-session respawn places the customer (Codex P2 on #2123). The domain 7 follow
  tick runs it too, which also catches an NPC that moved or left. This closes the NPC-TALK-1 follow-up: the conversation
  no longer waits for the speaker's next `say`.

## Excluded

- Admitted Dialogue walk-away lines: no Dialogue NPC has a talk book entry (NPC-TALK-1 excluded).
