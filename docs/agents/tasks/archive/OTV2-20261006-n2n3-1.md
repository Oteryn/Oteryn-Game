## Outcome

After admission the client builds a placeholder view from the JoinSnapshot (own position, `world_object_overlay` markers) drawn with the existing TileBatch and SpriteBatch. ClickWalk and arrow keys call `Session::step_retrying`; the step outcome and domain-1 and domain-2 deltas move the view. Any session error returns to login with `PublicClass::SessionUnavailable`. No new protocol, map loading, or creature/chat rendering.

Extensions beyond the packet's owned paths:

- `crates/session/src/lib.rs` (control-plane approved): re-export of the step and overlay types and `CommandStatus`, because the client may not depend on protocol-oteryn (ADR-0020).
- `apps/client/src/lib.rs`, `main.rs` (proposed to the control plane, no objection received): `ClientBootstrap::block_on`, `AdmittedSession::play_view` and `pump`, and `native_entry` returning the client with the admitted session so the winit shell can drive the session on the runtime its stream is bound to.

`windows_shell.rs` cannot be compiled on the Linux worker (no mingw for aws-lc); it was checked by reading and is covered by Windows CI.
