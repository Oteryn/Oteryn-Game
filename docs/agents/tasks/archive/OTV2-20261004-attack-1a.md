## Outcome

The pure half of ATTACK-1 (ATTACK-0 §4 and §5,
`docs/architecture/reviews/OTERYN_GAME_ATTACK0_ATTACK_TARGET_AND_AUTO_ATTACK_DECISION_2026-09-30.md`).
Nothing calls it yet; ATTACK-1b wires it into the Channel runtime and the GAME-ABILITY-01 pipeline.

- `combat/attack/target.rs`: per-actor attack state. Target admission (§3 refusals), §4 validity (out of
  range, other floor, protection zone and re-entry protection wait; dead, vanished or non-creature clear),
  the 2,000 ms auto-attack timer with `DEADLINE_STATE` catch-up (at most one swing per poll, the next
  deadline counts from execution, a target change keeps the interval), a stable swing sequence, the closed
  RNG purposes and the 60 s in-fight deadline (refreshed per hit, never shortened, kept when the target is
  cleared).
- `combat/attack/formulas.rs`: fist, melee and creature-melee damage and the defence range as
  `player_expression` trees for `spell/formula.rs` (no new engine; level term on the official 13.05 curve),
  plus the armor reduction range.
- `combat/attack/block.rs`: the time-based block budget (one per 1,000 ms, at most 2).
- `content/combat/attack_constants_v1.json`: the constants table (`PARITY_PENDING`), loaded by
  `include_str!` and checked at parse, like `content/movement/step_speed_v1.json`; no manifest or registry
  entry.

The module is a top-level `combat_attack` in `lib.rs`, not under `combat.rs`: `combat.rs` is also compiled
standalone by four `tests/*_postgres.rs` targets and the Foundation test crates, which have no
`crate::spell` (same reason as `combat_pickup`). The CP approved this (option a) on the BLOCKER.

Left to ATTACK-1b: the `ATTACK0-RL-03` resource-limit row, the RNG draws, charm hooks, the Ability
invocation, domain 10 and the logout blocker.
