## Outcome

CHAT-1a is the first part of CHAT-1 (CHAT-0 `CHAT0-LOCAL-PRIVATE-WORLD-ROOMS-V1`, allocation
#162 5916023254). It adds the pure local chat rules in `apps/game-server/src/chat/`. Nothing calls
them yet:

- **Text (§4):** the byte bound of 1,020 applies to the text as received; the text is trimmed and
  must then be 1 to 255 scalar values; control characters are refused (`CHAT0-RL-01`).
- **Speech (§3, Canary ranges of §2):** say reaches ±8 × ±6 on the speaker's floor. A whisper is
  shown in full on adjacent tiles and as `pspsps` elsewhere in say range. A yell reaches ±18 × ±14
  on the floors of `spectators.cpp:125-137`, upper-cased so that its length does not change.
- **Spam (§6):** a bucket of 4 lines, one back every 2.5 s. A line beyond the bucket is dropped and
  mutes for 5 s × n², where n counts the last 16 offences within 30 minutes. The bucket is full when
  the mute ends. `is_muted` is the read that the spell path needs.
- **Yell gates (§3, §6):** a 30 s cooldown; level 1 is refused, and so is a level below 20 without
  `premium_current`.
- **NPC greeting (§3):** whole-word match, multi-word greetings kept in order, the same floor
  within the talk range (`CHAT0-RL-08`, 4), the nearest NPC first and then the lowest actor id.

## Assumptions

- The yell floor rule follows the floor range of Canary `spectators.cpp:125-137` only. Canary's
  per-floor diagonal offset of the viewport is not applied.
- Yell upper-cases only the characters that stay one character, so the text bounds still hold
  (`ß` stays as it is).
- A line refused by the mute, the yell gate or the yell cooldown takes nothing from the bucket.
- The NPC talk range is Chebyshev distance on one floor.
