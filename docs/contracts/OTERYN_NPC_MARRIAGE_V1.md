## Character and consent owner

A Character is single, proposed, engaged or married. A proposal names an existing,
different Character in the same World and consumes one ring3004 and box9586.
Incoming proposals do not reserve their target. A target accepts by independently
performing the same confirmed `marry`/name operation naming the proposer; this
consumes the target's ring+box and changes both relations to engaged. An already
proposed, engaged or married actor cannot create another proposal. Engaged and
married relations must be reciprocal; no third-party overwrite is permitted.

The immutable proposal is durable consent, not a current session/controller
credential. Acceptance resolves the still-current proposal under both root locks;
an offline proposer may receive the agreed relationship and appearance effects.
Only the acting participant's current session-generation/lease/scope/node and
CharacterRevision authorize each new command. Its CharacterRevision advances
once, with the existing Character progression revision chain. Relation versions
advance once for each affected participant under roots locked in identity order.
No other actor/controller authority is acquired or restored by these effects.

A proposed actor may cancel: refund its exact ring+box and clear only its relation.
Engaged cancellation is absent in the donors and remains refused. Either engaged
participant may confirm celebration: both become married; the celebrant receives
one9585 engraved with the two committed display names and the UTC ceremony date.
Either married participant may confirm free divorce: clear both relations, with
no item refund, outfit removal or fee. Each stage is one atomic transaction.

## Receipts and recovery

CommandRef serializes before replay. The immutable receipt binds actor, NPC actor
identity/generation, original bounded talk bytes, operation and target. An exact
terminal replay precedes current conversation, input custody and fresh fence
checks; a conflicting binding writes nothing. Replay returns the original reply
and committed revision and never acquires present actor authority. Item effects,
inscription, account grants, relation versions and the acting Character revision
commit or roll back together. Recovery verifies each current relation's receipt
lineage and reciprocal pair invariant. No legacy spouse/storage global is read.
