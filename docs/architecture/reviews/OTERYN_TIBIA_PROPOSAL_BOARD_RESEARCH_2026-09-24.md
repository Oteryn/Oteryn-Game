# Tibia Proposal Board: player evidence for Oteryn product planning

- Research date: 2026-09-24
- Scope: targeted reading of public [Proposal Board](https://www.tibia.com/forum/?action=board&boardid=10) threads, their opening posts and selected replies; follow-up to [PR #571](https://github.com/Oteryn/Oteryn-Game/pull/571) and the [2026-09-11 product audit](OTERYN_EVOLVED_PR571_PRODUCT_AUDIT_2026-09-11.md).
- Status: **RESEARCH / PROPOSED RECOMMENDATIONS**. `IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORITY: NONE`; `PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY: NONE`.

## What was verified, and what was not

The 18 requests `A01–A18` in PR #571's QoL inventory reproduce the same 18 ideas in the same order as the opening post of [“18 Quality of Life Suggestions”](https://www.tibia.com/forum/?action=thread&threadid=4999611) by Fiers, dated 2026-08-15. This establishes the source for that ordered batch. It does **not** establish a source for the later `B01–B19`, `C01–C02`, or `D01–D18` batches: the inspected roadmap and QoL document contain descriptions and destinations, but no original thread IDs for those batches. Similar wording or overlap (`A09` and `C01`) is not proof of common origin.

Six independent, read-only thematic passes inspected representative threads spanning client/loot UX, economy, progression, combat, social/housing and board guidance. This is a **targeted qualitative sample**, not a census of the board, a representative survey or a complete reading of every page of the large threads. The “Last 30 Days” board view displayed 283 results across ten pages on the research date; the selected threads include older posts recently updated in that view. Thread view/reply counts are exposure/activity indicators; they cannot be read as votes. Some posts repeat their author's “up” message. Dates and thread statistics can change. The official [forum guide](https://www.tibia.com/gameguides/?section=forum&subtopic=manual) distinguishes public reading from posting rights. The official [proposal guidance](https://www.tibia.com/forum/?action=announcement&announcementid=32&boardid=10) asks for one focused topic with a problem, outcome and drawbacks. The [Forwarded Proposals](https://www.tibia.com/forum/?action=announcement&announcementid=62&boardid=10) list covers forwarding only through September 2015; absence there says nothing about newer proposals and is not a current acceptance register for Oteryn.

The first Oteryn Reference target remains the accepted **Global Tibia behavior after the 2026-07-28 server-save/maintenance boundary**, per [the protected reconciliation](../OTERYN_PRODUCT_PROFILE_REFERENCE_TARGET_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-09.md). A 2026-09 forum wish or current Tibia manual does not revise that target. “Shared UX” requires checking whether the feature changes only presentation, the information available to the player, command timing, or economic output. All proposals below are candidates for design and testing, not product decisions.

## Recommended first slices

1. **Presentation with unchanged authority:** loot channel and combat text filter. Acceptance: filtering affects only what the client displays; all server events and the canonical loot result remain unchanged. Test default behavior and crowded party fights.
2. **Explicit logistics:** quick stacking, inventory clarity and later loadouts. Quick stacking keeps the same pickup rights/capacity; loadouts require an explicit Game-owned operation that rejects invalid or unavailable sets without an unannounced partial result. Test combat timing, reconnect and retry semantics.
3. **Housing details under existing acceptance:** lighting and movable decor first, then bounded bed semantics. Use the already accepted Residence versus physical-house ownership choice.
4. **Research gates for economics/progression:** source-and-sink budget for NPC resale/supply/boss events; scenario matrix for stamina/death/proficiency; abuse model for PvP XP. No percentages, prices or cooldowns are frozen by this research.

The product goal remains a coherent path from finding an activity through preparation, group formation and safe settlement, as set out in the earlier audit. Presentation work can begin when its owning surface exists. Product-impacting operations must be evaluated as changes to action tempo, information, World supply and role balance rather than approved merely because players called them “QoL.” The [multichannel scope matrix](../MULTICHANNEL_SYSTEM_SCOPE_MATRIX.md) assigns corpse runtime to Channel, inventory and rewards to durable owners, and Market to World; the design for each operation must honor those boundaries.

## Outstanding evidence

- Original forum provenance for `B01–B19`, `C01–C02` and `D01–D18` remains unknown; request text similarity cannot fill the gap. `A01–A18` is the only batch mapped to one exact source here.
- This sample does not establish current player-population preference, exhaustive board coverage, complete later replies in every multipage thread, or the exact 2026-07-28 Reference behavior for each mechanic.
- It does not validate implementation state in server/client or authorize changes to PR #571. The [earlier 192-entry register](OTERYN_EVOLVED_PR571_DECISION_REGISTER_2026-09-11.json) remains an explicitly dated proposal inventory, not an accepted backlog.
