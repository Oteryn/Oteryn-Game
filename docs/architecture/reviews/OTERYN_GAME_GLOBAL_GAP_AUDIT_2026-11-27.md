### Ten gaps by player impact

1. **Personal housing acquisition.** A player cannot open an auction on a vacant house through the live bid route, and a pre-opened personal auction has no live settlement.
2. **Newhaven mainland progression.** Every new character who takes Anna’s ferry follows Crystal’s Targuna route and misses the Global destination for their vocation.
3. **Per-character and junior banking.** A shared account wallet changes who owns gold, prevents legitimate same-account transfers and bypasses Rookgaard’s economy rules.
4. **Market sell inventory and offer selection.** Players cannot list directly from Inbox or Supply Stash, and automatic matching can consume the specific offer they intended to accept.
5. **Guild war completion and payment.** Guilds can start a war and score kills, but there is no live match settlement, payout or outcome notification.
6. **Equipment tier preservation in the Market.** A catalogue filter cannot keep different tiers separate in orders, escrow, pricing or settlement.
7. **Cross-town Depot access.** Moving between cities changes which items a player can reach, affecting resupply, selling and travel.
8. **Party manual loot rights and loot messages.** Members can use quick-loot but are denied equivalent manual taking during exclusivity, and the party lacks the complete automatic kill-loot feed.
9. **Housing rent and transfer timing.** Arrears can run on the rent period instead of seven days, and a transfer can reset the due date, changing when players pay or lose a house.
