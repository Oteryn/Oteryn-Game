## Design and authority

- Use the existing World event owner and raid supervisor. Keep one global occurrence per game World across its Channels, keyed by server-save epoch, with city, robber and state (eligible → robber alive → bag outstanding → returned, or closed at save). Roll once per elapsed hour from authoritative World boot. Persist the boot basis, last checked hour and selection so Channel polls and restart retries cannot reroll an hour or duplicate a spawn. Select from the four sourced city pairs below.
- Project one robber and its verified escort on the normal map at event start. Anyone can engage solo without a dialogue prerequisite, party, instance or per-character cooldown. The committed death grants corpse loot to top damage, including an occurrence-bound tradeable bag. Double Loot can mint a second distinct bag. Keep banks closed and announcements active while the bag is outstanding.
- The affected banker gives the hideout clue and suspends banking. Both Thais bankers close together. Gate NPC dialogue and Bank preview, confirmation and fresh settlement on the same occurrence so a pre-closure prompt cannot bypass suspension. Existing committed receipts still replay.
- Board USE in the Adventurers' Guild and Thais Towncryer read that same state. The Board says “Several banks in major coastal towns are being robbed! The thieves are still on the loose!” Towncryer says “Hear ye! Hear ye! Stand and deliver!” Both omit the city.
- At an affected banker, “hi” recognizes the current bag, asks about the recovered gold and binds that bag and occurrence to the live conversation. “yes” consumes the bag and pays 10 platinum coins (1,000 gp). The first committed return reopens the affected bank services, clears announcements and enables the success greeting through save. No new robber after return through save.
- Reuse the fenced NPC reward, item custody and account achievement owners. Under the current session-generation fence, revalidate banker, conversation, World/epoch, bag mint and custody under locks. In one transaction consume the bag, mint coins, advance the credited return count, grant Honest Finder on the first and Goldhunter on the fifth, and transition the World state on the first return. Bind the receipt to World, epoch, bag instance and the exact request. Serialize fresh hand-ins and save against the same epoch state. Reconcile the committed receipt on replay or lost acknowledgement. Any failed settlement, including insufficient payout capacity, rolls back the whole hand-in.
- Committed server save closes the occurrence, reopens banks, retires surviving event spawns, clears greetings and announcements, and expires remaining bags. Use item custody retirement for occurrence-bound bags across custody locations, including offline holdings. Retire by indexed occurrence-bound mint identities. The epoch fence rejects expired bags even while retirement is being projected.

Plan **one migration** from reserved **3313–3316** for World robbery lifecycle and settlement bindings, reusing the existing reward receipt and achievement owners. New command or capability needed **no**. Existing NPC talk, World USE, event query and trade routes carry the behavior.
