# Critical hits, leech and skill advance

The official manual §combat 5.3.5 confirms independent chance/intensity/duration and applicability to spells/runes. It does not state exact numeric tier ranges or a complete damage-to-leech formula.

Current Wiki intrinsic critical chance (5%) and bonus damage (10%), leech arithmetic, per-vocation skill multipliers, magic-level formulas, rounding and multi-target rules remain **UNKNOWN for gameplay admission** until official or controlled evidence is supplied. A server implementation, including Canary/Crystal, is not independent proof of a number.
