# W11 server journeys

CrystalServer revision `201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8` supplies the executable donor rules. The archived Tibia Wiki spoilers, Soul War revision 2025-10-16 and Rotten Blood revision 2025-06-28, supply the player route and call out retail behaviour where it differs from the scripts. The access bosses in each quest can be fought in different orders. The tables use the spoilers' order. [Crystal Soul War configuration](https://github.com/zimbadev/crystalserver/blob/201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8/data-global/lib/quests/soul_war.lua) · [Crystal Rotten Blood scripts](https://github.com/zimbadev/crystalserver/tree/201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8/data-global/scripts/quests/rotten_blood_quest) · [Soul War spoiler](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_War_Quest%2FSpoiler) · [Rotten Blood spoiler](https://tibia.fandom.com/wiki/Rotten_Blood_Quest%2FSpoiler)

At each lever, Crystal checks the waiting pads, room occupancy, participant minimum, level, individual cooldown and any encounter-specific admission check. A successful pull starts the fight timer and places the participants in the room. The default fight limit is 20 minutes. ## Soul War

| Step | Entry, gate and resulting state |
| --- | --- |
| Zarganash and the hub | Complete Feaster of Souls and travel via Vengoth to Zarganash, then take the southwest hub portal. Tell A Flickering Soul `task` and `yes` to earn teleport access. The five hunt portals check access and level 250. |
| Claustrophobic Inferno → Malice | Take the left fog portal. Cross the sandtimer trigger and survive three successive two-minute raids, taking the stairs between them. The last fog portal reaches the Malice lever. Fight through the soul cage cycle and use the white tiles to survive the flood. |
| Mirrored Nightmare → Greed | Take the rightmost fog portal. Use mirror 33782 to make a Mirror Image when the source cooldown allows, then hit Mirror Images and defeat 25 apparitions of each vocation. The temple portal admits the character to the Greed lever. Kill five Greedbeasts to open the vulnerable phase. Keep Soul Spheres from reaching Greed and use vortices to intercept Weak Souls and Soulsnatchers. |
| Ebb and Flow → Spite | Take the middle fog portal and defeat 20 Hazardous Phantoms across the flood cycle. The source step at `33950, 31109, 8` checks the counter and sends the character to the Spite lever. Stomp the recurring soulfire and avoid the Pool of Tears. |
| Furious Crater → Cruelty | Take the fourth fog portal. Hit the source creatures and step on their Pulsating Energy. Pass the three floor gates at 40, 55 and 70 energy and reach Cruelty's lever. Lead Poor Souls through the Greedy Eye's beam and use their Mortal Essence on the Greedy Maw to limit the boss's damage resistance. |
| Rotten Wasteland → Hatred | Take the second fog portal. Use the blue, green, orange and violet shrines and pass the west gate to Hatred's lever. Manage the Burning Hatred fire and Dread's Torment during the fight. |
| Hub → Megalomania | Each qualifying vice kill sets its boss flag and grants a taint. The first taint starts the 14-day chain clock. The final portal at `33611, 31430, 10` checks level 250 and all five flags. The lever starts purple. Four Aspect of Power kills open green. Blue channels Annihilation and remains vulnerable. The fight also uses splinters, Necromantic Remains, torment and white tiles. Terminal victory records completion and resets the vice flags and first-taint clock. |
| Report and collect | Report `task` to A Flickering Soul for the Revenant outfit. Victory unlocks the reward portal at `33621, 31416, 10`. Use the chest at `33620, 31400, 10` to claim one random Soul Set item from source IDs 34082–34099 per character. The exit portal returns to Thais. |

Crystal registers an effective 20-hour cooldown for the five vices and 72 hours for purple Megalomania. The spoiler gives 68 hours for the latter. The donor contains the five hunt routes, counter and lever scripts, completion dialogue and chest script. ## Rotten Blood

| Step | Entry, gate and resulting state |
| --- | --- |
| Drefia → temple | At level 250, tell Torkada `time` (Crystal) or `expedition` (spoiler), then `mission` and `yes` to accept the mission and unlock the temple door. |
| Sacrificial Bloodshade → Blood Vestibule | Ask the first Bloodshade about `quest`. At the sacrificial plate, present a Bloody Tear to advance access from 1 to 2, then consume one tear each for 2 → 3 and 3 → 4. The alternate `detour`, `yes`, `yes` route costs 5,000,000 gold and grants stage 4. The blood entrance at `32953, 32398, 9` requires level 250 and stage 4. Insufficient offerings send the player back and can inflict physical damage. Take the vestibule exit to return to the temple. |
| Vestibule acknowledgement | Ask the inner Bloodshade about `quest` for the two pairs of twins. This advances access from 4 to 5. The four Spheric Teleporters lead to the hunts. The spoiler describes one passive charge every 20 seconds and a fresh count for each run. |
| Jaded Roots → Murcion | Enter the hunt, build 100 Rotten Charges, and reach the Murcion lever. Organic Acid on a Small Spore Reservoir grants one charge and a Spore Reservoir grants fifteen. Manage the mushrooms and Bloodjaws. |
| Darklight Core → Vemiath | Enter the hunt, build 100 Rotten Charges, and reach the Vemiath lever. Use Unstable or Yellow Darklight Matter on blood pools for one or three charges, or a Darklight Obelisk for fifteen. Use a Bloodjaw to discharge the recurring charge and open a vulnerability window with two retaliatory attacks. |
| Gloom Pillars → Chagorz | Enter the hunt, build 100 Rotten Charges, and reach the Chagorz lever. A Darklight Obsidian Axe on a pillar grants one charge and an Obelisk grants fifteen. Manage dark energy and the boss's shield and pillar mechanics. |
| Putrefactory → Ichgahal | Use entrance action 34120. The donor uses the Putrefactory area counter, capped at 100. Qualifying action 47953 uses add 20 ticks. Action 34065 checks that threshold before teleporting to the lever. The spoiler also describes charges from Lichen Gobblers and Spore Reservoirs. Manage the mushroom and Bloodjaw mechanics. |
| Bakragore | Each child grants its chain flag and one regular taint. The final lever requires all four flags and four taints. Optional essence sacrifices replace Bloodjaws with the corresponding echoes. Bakragore combines mushrooms, mould and darkfield tracks, pillars, and the Bloodjaw discharge cycle. Failure removes two matching child flags and taints. Victory clears the regular chain, retains the final taint and records completion. |
| Rewards | Victory grants the Decaying Defender base outfit. Bag You Covet is chance-based. The spoiler also describes post-victory Crimson Offerings. Crystal's altar exchange consumes five Tainted Hearts, five Darklight Hearts and 50,000,000 gold for one bag. |

The four children use 20-hour cooldowns, with Vemiath and Ichgahal inheriting the default. Crystal's canonical Bakragore lever uses a 68-hour cooldown. The second lever in the donor uses 20 hours at a distinct placement. The spoiler describes 25 minutes for Bakragore and the Oteryn activity policy uses 20 minutes. ## Published-world blocker

At the requested base, reading every shard named in `content/world/npc-placements/index.json` finds no placement for `oteryn:npc.bloodshade_rotten`. Crystal's world NPC XML places it at `34069, 31982, 14`. This NPC owns the stage 4 → 5 acknowledgement. The quest definition and NPC dialogue exist, and the existing progression test places an actor beside the character in its isolated fixture. The World compiler's pin-check passed. Decoding its artifact with the World Bundle reader confirmed the absence among 1,138 published placements. Publishing the donor placement requires the NPC placement family and regenerated world pin, which are in the PR #3487 exclusion list. ## Soul War reward portal regression

The routed case `w11_soul_war_reward_portal_refuses_before_completion` booted the pinned World, admitted a level-250 character and reached its PostgreSQL assertions. With the base handler it committed a north STEP and left the character at `33621, 31416, 10`. Crystal returns the character to `33621, 31417, 10` while preserving facing. This case covers the physical gate before completion. The existing chest USE and outfit tests cover the reward settlement. [Crystal reward portal](https://github.com/zimbadev/crystalserver/blob/201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8/data-global/scripts/quests/soul_war/moveevent-teleport_entrance_reward.lua)

With the completion check in `soul_war_world.rs`, the same routed case passed with its database marker. It asserted the return cell, preserved facing, identical durable quest state, and no pending reward. The post-completion crossing and full in-game chains remain outside this case's assertions.
