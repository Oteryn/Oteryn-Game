# Damage parity

Area: player damage (attack runes and attack spells). Date: 2026-11-16. Format: `README.md` in this directory.

Sources: Crystal = `zimbadev/crystalserver` commit `201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8`, file:line below; REPO = our content. Crystal is an OTS hypothesis, not client measurement. The Global import and Crystal are the working basis; client measurement decides. No Wiki evidence was used and Wiki alone changes no value.
Confidence: REFERENCE throughout (not measured). MATCH/MISMATCH compare the stated Crystal reference, not a claim of measured Global parity. UNKNOWN means an absent explicit source value or an unverified formula/area equivalence; both representations are retained. No content fixes are authorized by this table.

Ours is the complete spell-family catalog registered by `content/abilities/definitions/index.json`; bundles carry their own abilities, effects and formula dependencies. Rune items were cross-checked in `content/items/definitions/spell-native-profiles.json` (rune consumption and item bindings). Rune rows describe use, not conjuring; mana absent from a Crystal registration is UNKNOWN rather than assumed zero. Charges are registration charges, not item-count consumption. `P` is base power, `L` level, `ML` magic level; `level_base_damage_healing` is the catalog function, not an assumed L/5 coefficient. Native/conditional branches are retained as structured values when a scalar comparison is unsafe. Formula rows remain UNKNOWN pending complete helper/branch equivalence verification.
