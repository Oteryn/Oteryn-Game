# Winter Update 2026: official forum posts on new content (read 2026-11-14)

Source: the official test-server forum (board "Winter Update 2026"), threads 561-564 and 566-570, all pages, CipSoft Game Content Designer posts only. Dates are dd.mm.yyyy. Entries in each group run from oldest to newest, so a later entry replaces an earlier value.

## Banner's Return

### Quest (thread 566)
- 09.11: The quest starts in Bounac: ask Onfroi about "banner". Requirements:
  - "Order of the Stag" is completed;
  - Captain Banner was met during the battle on the Isle of Ada.
  Revisiting her on the Isle of Ada is not needed if the warlock is already defeated. The quest area is Onyx Massif and the Red Gullet.
- 10.11: A ladder to the north will be added in a later update. A quest log typo is fixed.
- 11.11:
  - Onfroi now reveals Boson's identity after the player talks to Kesar.
  - Ladders are fixed.
  - Builders count in a party hunt.
  - Orbs: use one on each of the four pillars by element. The fiery orb needs a one-time charge, but an uncharged fiery orb still completes the quest.
  - "Ashen Beast" is a special encounter, not in the Bosstiary.
  - Summoning the Onyx Beast resets the Builder kill counter.
- 12.11: The Onyx Beast count and its required kills are now displayed. The Blood Onyx Core requirements are clearer. No items are planned beyond the current package. Red Gullet will likely stop counting toward Onyx Massif exploration; only the surrounding areas count.
- 13.11: Collecting kills beyond the amount one Onyx Beast needs is not intended. Scorch status visibility changed.

### Hunting grounds (thread 568)
- 09.11:
  - Onyx Massif: manageable density, slightly easier than Bloodfire Gorge (Isle of Ada).
  - Darkfire Gorge: higher density, slightly harder than Bloodfire Gorge.
  - Red Gullet: the hardest. Builders (stronger omega knights) at lower density.
  - The scorch level sets how many blood cinders a Red Gullet kill gives. Red Gullet creatures have no regular loot.
  - The Ash Sentry NPC exchanges blood cinders for new valuables, monster traps, decorative objects, Gullet-only rings and more.
- 11.11:
  - Red Gullet access: Builder kills count for every player who dealt enough damage, not only the top damage dealer.
  - Omega Moonbreaker: weapon rack summon range reduced.
  - Onyx Beast summon: Builder kills lowered from 250 to 50 on the test server only. Builders in Darkfire Gorge do not count.
  - Gullet rings will not work outside the Red Gullet.
- 13.11:
  - Killing the Onyx Beast resets the kill count of every player who gains a scorch level from it, the summoner included.
  - The test server assistant's voodoo skull sets the scorch level to 4 (test server only).
  - Omega knight stats are under review.

### Boss: Banner's Nemesis (thread 569)
- 09.11:
  - An Archfoe boss with a 20-hour challenge cooldown. Captain Banner fights alongside the players.
  - The gear drop has a pity tracker that applies only until the first drop. After that only the base chance applies, plus boss bonus and boss scaling loot.
- 11.11: Onyx Beast summon progress counts for every player who attacked Builders. The Blood Onyx Core message shows the remaining kills.
- 12.11: The boss no longer dies after all players leave the room. Volcanic charge no longer damages non-player creatures in the room.
- 13.11:
  - Omega Shades also attack Captain Banner.
  - The boss and Captain Banner remove magic fields.
  - Fixed: the chain spell did not trigger after the eruption wave (difficulty 10 and higher).
  - Dying or logging out does not reset the kill count, and blood cinders are kept either way.
  - Trapping and blocking at the entrance are being fixed.
  - Fix in the next patch: Captain Banner stayed at 1 HP for most of the fight (reported at difficulty 2).

## I Smell a Rat

### Quest (thread 563)
- 09.11: The quest starts at Hushwell Asylum, on the road from Thais to Kazordoon.
- 10.11: Gathering musk from summoned rats is allowed. Greed Marks are not given out freely; the labyrinth shortcut needs 30 Greed Marks.
- 13.11:
  - A pickaxe creates a resonance that the guard on the other side reacts to.
  - Secret Service tools also work here; no more tools will be added.
  - After killing all bosses the player has enough Greed Marks to bypass the labyrinth. Spending the 30 marks means waiting 20 hours to retry.

### Hunting grounds (thread 562)
- 09.11: Four areas:
  - Rattica Outskirts: mostly giant rats; an introduction area that also works as a hunting ground.
  - Rattica Warrens: spellcasting hoarder rats and ranged greed carrier rats; comparable to Hyaena Lairs.
  - Rattica Underbelly: swarms of hoarder rats and greed carrier rats; comparable to Hyaena Lairs.
  - Greed Dimension: behind the boss; greed collected here feeds the Greed Monger; about as hard as Asura Palace.
- New mechanic, Frenzy: a frenzied monster or boss deals more damage the fewer hit points it has. A new icon shows it. Timing and strength depend on the creature; giant rats use it most.

### Bosses: Temple of Greed (thread 564)
- 09.11:
  - Encounters are for teams of two.
  - The lever cooldown of 20 hours applies only to the player who pulls the lever. A player on lever cooldown can still join other attempts without limit.
  - The reward cooldown of 20 hours applies to all participants.
- 12.11, balancing:
  - Ratasha: cheese of death is cast more often; some spells changed in shape, damage and frequency.
  - Rathilda: filthy pet slime has a longer cast range; the slime is sturdier.
  - Greedking: personal greed level drops faster; collecting greed does more damage while personal greed is 10 or higher; greed tentacles are slightly faster.
  - Boss bonus does not apply to chest loot; chest loot chances are somewhat higher instead.
  - Levers now explain why the player cannot proceed.
- 13.11, next patch, Greedking: damage is spread more evenly (fewer spikes), mana drain is reduced, and its effective health pool is lower because some sensitivities were raised.
