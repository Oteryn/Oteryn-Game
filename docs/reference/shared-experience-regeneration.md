# Shared experience and vital regeneration

The live official manual was retrieved on 2026-10-18. Shared experience requires `3 * minimum_level >= 2 * maximum_level`, distance at most 30 tiles from the leader, and at most one floor difference. Its bonus is 20/30/60/100% for 1/2/3/4+ distinct vocations. These primary-source values take precedence over the conflicting community list; both observations remain recorded. The exact activity timeout still lacks official numeric support.

Official stamina and soul rules are recorded in their ruleset packets. Promotion sets the soul cap to 200 instead of 100. Numeric HP/MP intervals for all five vocations and their promotions are retained as structured community observations, not discarded or admitted as official formulas. Food durations cover 75 exact own-item IDs; their gameplay qualification, the food cap, and exact active-combat stamina drain remain UNKNOWN without official or controlled evidence.
