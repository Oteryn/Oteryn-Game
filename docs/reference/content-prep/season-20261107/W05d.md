## Concrete dependency

Crystal `201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8`,
`data-global/scripts/lib/a_piece_of_cake_config.lua:249-292`, creates bridge
floor item **48227**. That target has no canonical definition in
`content/world/definitions/reference.json` or EXACT entry in
`imports/crystalserver/bindings/items.json`. The current native admission records
it as `EVENT_ITEM_CANONICAL_IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE`; the check is in
`tools/content-migration/event_item_profiles.py:32-33,158-161,272-286`.

The live owner answer was re-read on November 7:
[ANSWER 6549960114](https://github.com/Oteryn/Oteryn-Game/pull/2781#issuecomment-6549960114):
"Keep 48227 typed unavailable" and "the Cake bridge projection goes under Left
out as a dependency on the canonical Item identity/map owner." It also rejects
a per-item source suffix or sorted-reference exception. Physical appearance
facts alone do not supply the missing identity. The exact pinned source evidence
is retained by `tools/content-schema/native-gameplay/event_item_identity.py`.

Completing Cake therefore needs the canonical Item identity/map owner to supply
an admitted bridge definition or an explicit replacement specification. A
seasonal producer cannot create a valid native bridge reference today. Allocating
a migration or changing the combat consumer does not resolve this dependency.
