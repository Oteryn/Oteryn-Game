### Actual speed functions and exact quotations

All paths in this subsection are Crystal
`201189f871d23597b82caf88fd611454a2ddefb8` unless stated otherwise.

- `src/creatures/players/player.cpp:7222-7231`, `Player::updateBaseSpeed`:
  line 7228 is
  `baseSpeed = static_cast<uint16_t>(vocation->getBaseSpeed() + (level - 1));`.
  The alternate staff flag sets `PLAYER_MAX_SPEED` at line 7230. There is no
  discovery progress argument, storage read or additive discovery term.
- `src/creatures/creature.cpp:1528-1530`, `Creature::getSpeed`:
  `return std::clamp(baseSpeed + varSpeed, 0, static_cast<int>(std::numeric_limits<uint16_t>::max()));`.
  `Creature::setSpeed`, lines 1532-1539, checks the maximum and assigns
  `varSpeed = varSpeedDelta;`.
- `src/game/game.cpp:7085-7095`, `Game::changeSpeed`, and
  `:7106-7115`, `Game::changePlayerSpeed`: both calculate
  `int32_t varSpeed = ...->getSpeed() - ...->getBaseSpeed();`, then
  `varSpeed += varSpeedDelta;`, then `...->setSpeed(varSpeed);`.
  They transmit the resulting step speed; they do not derive a discovery
  bonus. `Game::setCreatureSpeed` at `:7097-7103` directly sets base speed.
- `src/creatures/creature.hpp:210-211` returns `getSpeed()` from
  `getStepSpeed()`. Its `updateCalculatedStepSpeed` at `:902-910` uses
  `std::floor((Creature::speedA * log(stepSpeed + Creature::speedB) + Creature::speedC) + .5)`
  and clamps the calculated result to at least 1. The constants are
  `857.36`, `261.29`, `-4795.01` at `:84-86`.
- `src/creatures/creature.cpp:1492-1510`, `Creature::getStepDuration`:
  `auto duration = std::floor(1000 * walk.groundSpeed / walk.calculatedStepSpeed);`
  followed by `std::ceil(duration / SERVER_BEAT) * SERVER_BEAT`, then the
  direction/monster movement cost. This is the ordinary movement conversion
  already represented by `crates/gameplay/characters/src/speed.rs`, not a
  discovery-progress formula.
