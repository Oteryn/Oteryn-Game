## 1.1 What Is Tibia?

Free-to-play hardcore fantasy MMORPG with retro aesthetics, one of Europe's oldest and most successful online RPGs [server/platform]. Players assume knight, paladin, sorcerer, druid, or monk roles [server]. Death carries severe penalties [server]. Endless character progression drives immersive gameplay [server]

## 1.2 Why Play Tibia?

**Free-to-play model** [platform]: No mandatory costs; optional Premium/Tibia Coins purchases enhance experience but remain voluntary [platform]. F2P and Premium players equally welcome [platform]

**Accessibility** [platform]: Client ~30MB (Windows/Linux/Mac); low system requirements; ~5-minute setup [platform]

**Huge world** [platform]: Hundreds of dungeons, hundreds of unique items, continuous expansion, endless exploration [platform]

**Character customization** [platform]: 5 vocations with distinct playstyles, multiple skins, dozens of spells, varied equipment, unlimited skill training [platform]

**Community** [platform]: 500,000+ active players worldwide; cross-continental interaction, trading, grouping, PvP [platform]

## 1.3 The Aim of the Game

No final end-goal; multiple playstyles: social interaction, exploration/discovery, character progression. Endless character improvement paths (equipment collection, skill training). Perpetual novelty as world expands [server/platform]

## 1.4 What Do You Need to Play Tibia?

**Windows** [client]:
- OS: Windows 10 Version 1809 (64-bit) or newer [client]
- Visual C++ Redistributable 2015-2022 [client]
- GPU: 128MB; DirectX 11 or OpenGL 2.1 (or software fallback) [client]
- CPU: 2.5GHz Intel Core i3+ equivalent [client]
- Storage: 350MB free [client]
- Internet: 28.8k+ connection [platform]

**Linux/Mac** [client]:
- 64-bit only [client]
- Unsupported; no platform-specific bug fixes provided [client]

## 1.5 Two Things to Keep in Mind When Playing Tibia

**Rules** [platform]: Tibia Rules define acceptable conduct; CipSoft enforces via penalty system; violations result in progressive punishment [platform]

**Security** [platform]: Character represents real account value; account theft risk exists; observe security guidelines (strong password, recovery key, authenticator, phishing awareness) [platform]
