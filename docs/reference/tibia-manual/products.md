## 6.3.1 Consumables: Blessings, Casks, Kegs, Potions, Exercise Weapons, Runes

### Blessings

Reduce death penalty on character [server]. Each character can hold up to 5 charges per blessing type [server].

**Regular Blessings** [server]:
- 7 types exist (purchasable individually or as bundle) [server]
- With all 7: up to 100% item loss prevention, up to 56% skill/experience loss reduction (44.17% on Retro Hardcore PvP) [server]
- Red/black-skulled characters lose all equipment and PvE blessings regardless [server]
- First charge from NPCs; additional charges (2-5) only via Store [server]
- Cannot exceed 5-charge maximum per type [server]

**PvP Blessing ("Twist of Fate")** [server]:
- Shields regular blessings and Amulet of Loss in PvP fights [server]
- Death in PvP: no charges consumed from regular blessings/Amulet if PvP blessing active [server]
- Non-applicable on Retro Open/Retro Hardcore PvP worlds [server]
- Red/black-skulled characters bypass protection but don't lose PvP blessing charge [server]
- Up to 5 charges, purchasable as full package [server]

**Death Redemption** [server]:
- Restores skill/experience lost due to inadequate blessing coverage at death [server]
- Purchasable within 24 hours post-death only [server]
- Only most recent unresolved death displayed [server]
- Unavailable on Retro Hardcore PvP worlds [server]

### Casks, Kegs, Potions

Consumables available for in-game store purchase [server]. Character must meet vocation/level requirements [server]. Cannot purchase at protection zone block or with battle sign active [client].

- Health potions [server]
- Mana potions [server]
- Spirit potions (restore both health and mana) [server]
- Kegs (500-potion capacity, portable) [server]
- Casks (1000-potion capacity, house-only; usable by all house guests) [server]
- Capacity limits enforced [server]
- Store-purchased potions/kegs are character-bound (non-tradeable) [server]

### Exercise Weapons

Train skills rapidly via exercise dummies [server]. Vocation-specific types:

- Exercise wand/rod (magic level) [server]
- Exercise bow (distance fighting) [server]
- Exercise axe/sword/club (corresponding fighting skill) [server]

Three quality tiers: 500 uses (exercise), 1800 uses (enhanced), 14,400 uses (masterful) [server]

### Runes

Purchasable rune packs for hunts [server]. Character must meet level/magic level requirements [server]. Cannot purchase at protection zone block or with battle sign active [client]. Store-purchased runes are character-bound (non-tradeable) [server].

## 6.3.2 Bundles

Predefined product combinations (e.g., mount + outfit + decoration) [server]. Technical limitation: bundles cannot be refunded, even if individual products within them are refundable under special circumstances [server].

## 6.3.3 Cosmetics: Mounts, Outfits

Both usable by free-to-play and Premium players [server]. Per-character purchase required for each character using the item [server].

**Mounts** [server]:
- Purchased as travel companions instead of earning via quests [server]
- Multiple colors available [server]

**Outfits** [server]:
- Fashion cosmetics [server]
- Multiple colors/styles available [server]

## 6.3.4 Houses

All house equipment is character-bound (non-tradeable) and owner-only unwrappable [server]. On house loss or transfer, wrapped items auto-return to inbox [server].

### Decorations [server]
Cosmetic and pet items for home customization. Non-tradeable. Owner-unwrappable only.

### Furniture [server]
Reusable home décor pieces. Non-tradeable. Owner-unwrappable only.

### Beds [server]
- Restore soul/mana/hit points [server]
- Train skills via usage [server]
- Two-box set, only unwrappable by purchaser (owner of house) [server]
- Single-user only; Premium status required to use [server]
- Non-tradeable; auto-wrapped and returned to inbox on house loss [server]

### Upgrades [server]
Functional house additions: mailbox, reward shrine, exercise dummy, imbuing shrine. Owner-unwrappable; usable by all house guests [server]. Non-tradeable; auto-wrapped on house loss [server].

### Hirelings [server]
NPCs placed in houses; maximum 10 per character. Gender and name customizable at purchase [server]. Names subject to Tibia Rules enforcement; random replacement on violation [server].

**Hireling Apprentice** [server]: Greets guests and sells basic items [server]

**Apprenticeships** [server]: Purchased to unlock hireling services:
- Banker: bank/guild bank access [server]
- Trader: city/trade goods inventory [server]
- Steward: stash access [server]
- Cook: food preparation (fee-based) [server]

All hirelings of same character share unlocked services [server]

### Hireling Dresses [server]
Cosmetic outfits for hirelings. Ownership limits outfit allocation (two hirelings = two outfit copies needed for same dress) [server]

### Hireling Services [server]
**Name Change** [server]: Rename hireling; subject to Tibia Rules; cancellable if name unavailable [server]
**Sex Change** [server]: Toggle hireling gender [server]

Visible only if player owns hirelings [server]

## 6.3.5 Boosts

XP experience multiplier during hunting [server]. Duration tracked as stamina-only (paused below 14-hour stamina) [server]. Maximum 5 boosts per character between server saves; price increases per purchase [server]. Cannot stack multiple boosts simultaneously [server]. Stacks additively with double-XP weekends and other bonuses [server].

## 6.3.6 Extras: Extra Services and Useful Things

### Character World Transfer (Regular and Express) [server]

Allows character migration to different game world [server]. Restricted by numerous conditions:

**Ownership restrictions** [server]:
- No red/black skull [server]
- Cannot be guild leader [server]
- No open Market offers involving Tibia Coins [server]
- Must be on main continent (not Newhaven/Rookgaard) [server]

**Frequency restrictions** [server]:
- Regular transfer: once per 6 months [server]
- Express transfer: for characters already transferred within 6 months [server]

**Automatic consequences on transfer** [server]:
- Guild membership removed (pick up items from guild hall first) [server]
- Marriage dissolved [server]
- All Market offers cancelled; sold items sent to inbox [server]
- Pending house bid/transfer cancelled [server]
- Rented house forfeited [server]

**Preserved on transfer** [server]: Bank account, depot, stash, inbox, skills, spells, quest flags [server]

**PvP-type restrictions** [server]:
- Optional PvP → Optional PvP only [server]
- Open/Retro Open PvP → Open/Retro Open PvP or Optional PvP [server]
- Hardcore/Retro Hardcore PvP → any world [server]
- Transfer to less-strict PvP worlds allowed from any world (except locked) [server]
- Locked worlds: inter-locked transfers only; movement to less-strict type possible [server]
- Hardcore PvP → Hardcore PvP blocked if character visited Calva/Calvera [server]
- BattlEye protection must match: protected-from-start ↔ protected-from-start [server]
- Blocked worlds: cannot transfer in; can transfer out [server]

**Premium-world restriction** [server]: Transfer to Premium world requires active Premium status [server]

**In-game execution** [server]: Travel to Travora (1000-gold ticket via harbor captain, valid 24h), consult NPC Sharon for transfer status check, proceed to platform, log out to trigger transfer at next server save (up to 24h completion) [server]

### Main Character Change [server]
Designate a different character as account's primary character [server]. Cannot select hidden or deletion-scheduled characters [server]

### Character Name Change [server]
Rename active character in Store [server]. Must obey Tibia Rules (violation = namelock or punishment) [server]. Name availability checked at purchase; rejected names trigger cancellation without coin charge [server]. Old name reserved for 6 months [server]. Post-rename: client restart required; house access/door rights must be regranted by owner [server]

### Character Sex Change [server]
Toggle character gender [server]. Instant application [server]. Character reverts to default outfit; earned/purchased outfits remain available [server]

### Useful Things [server]

**Prey Wildcard** [server]: Lock active prey, reroll bonuses, toggle automatic reroll, select custom prey. Maximum 52 owned [server]

**Gold Converter** [server]: Convert 100 gold ↔ 1 platinum or 100 platinum ↔ 1 crystal. Up to 500 uses per unit [server]

**Magic Gold Converter** [server]: Automatic version of above; up to 500 uses [server]

**Permanent Prey Slot** [server]: Unlock additional character prey slot (permanent) [server]

**Permanent Weekly Task Expansion** [server]: Unlock +3 Kill Tasks and +3 Delivery Tasks for character [server]

**Temple Teleport** [server]: Instant teleport to character's home temple [server]

**Instant Reward Access** [server]: Claim daily reward from any location (bypass shrine travel) [server]

**Charm Expansion** [server]: Free account: 2 Charms. Premium: 6 Charms. Expansion: unlock all simultaneously. Max 2 per creature (one minor, one major). 25% discount on remove/reset-all. Each charm one-per-creature [server]

**Gold Pouch** [server]: Carry unlimited gold/platinum/crystal coins (up to capacity limit). NPC purchases deduct from pouch first [server]
