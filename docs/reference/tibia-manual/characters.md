## 5.1 Characters (overview)

- [both] A character is the player avatar; some traits are fixed at creation (name, gender, world), others evolve during play.

## 5.1.1 Experience Points and Levels

- [server] Level advance is automatic once accumulated XP crosses the threshold for the next level; server must track an XP-to-level curve.
- [server] Sub-level-50 characters get a level-scaling XP bonus on kills; bonus shrinks as level rises toward 50 (manual gives no exact formula/numbers — qualitative only).
- [server] PvP kills grant zero experience (exception: Retro Hardcore worlds, see §combat 5.3.11.c).
- [both] Level gates access to some quests (exact gates are per-quest data, not in this section).
- [server] Traits that scale with level: max HP, max mana, capacity, walking speed, magic-attack/heal power (see 5.1.2–5.1.4 for specifics).

## 5.1.2 Hit Points and Mana

- [server] Max HP and max mana both rise every level; from level 8 onward the HP/mana split per level is vocation-dependent (fighters gain more HP, casters more mana) — see vocation table in 5.1.9.
- [server] 0 HP = death (see 5.1.11). 0 mana = cannot cast any spell.
- [server] HP/mana regenerate automatically over time; regen rate increases while "fed" (well-fed condition).
- [both] Healing spells and health potions restore HP instantly; mana potions restore mana instantly.
- [server] HP/mana can never exceed the level/vocation-determined maximum.
- [server] HP/mana regeneration is fully disabled while standing in a protection zone (e.g. depot, house) — no numeric rate given.

## 5.1.3 Monk Exclusive: Harmony, Virtues and Serenity

- [server] Monk-only resource "Harmony", generated by casting specific attack spells; caps at 5 stored.
- [server] Certain powerful monk spells consume all stored Harmony for a greatly boosted effect (heal AoE, single-target burst, or multi-target hit — player's choice of spell).
- [server] Promotion unlocks 3 Virtues; exactly one is active at a time and gives a passive effect.
- [server] All virtues share a base passive: monk heals self or an ally (no extra mana cost) whenever Harmony is generated or consumed.
- [server] Virtue of Harmony: boosts power of Harmony-consuming spells.
- [server] Virtue of Justice: boosts fist-fighting skill.
- [server] Virtue of Sustain: boosts healing effects generally.
- [server] "Virtue bonus": each vocation in the monk's party gets a unique bonus tied to its role (healer/damage/tank/caster) while a virtue is active — but only while the monk is Serene.
- [server] Serene state triggers when either: (a) no other party member is visible on screen, or (b) 5 or fewer adjacent tiles are occupied by creatures/characters (tile occupancy count, not creature count).
- [server] While Serene: virtue effects double; auto-attacks (melee) deal bonus damage. Exact multipliers not given.

## 5.1.4 Soul Points and Stamina

- [server] Soul and stamina are the only two character stats that do NOT scale with level.
- [server] Soul points are spent to cast rune spells and some item-conjuring spells (ammo, food).
- [server] Soul point cap: 100 normal, 200 promoted [server].
- [server] Soul regen trigger: a single kill granting XP ≥ character's own level starts a soul-regen window lasting 4 minutes.
- [server] Soul regen rate: 1 point / 2 minutes (normal); 1 point / 15 seconds (promoted).
- [server] Sleeping in a bed grants +1 soul point per 15 minutes of sleep.
- [server] Stamina counter: max 42 hours; at 0 stamina no more XP gain from hunting.
- [server] Stamina tiers: first 3 hours (hours 0–3, i.e. "up to hour 39" remaining) grant Premium players +50% XP per kill; last 14 hours of the 42 give only 50% XP per kill.
- [server] No-loot rule: if the top-damage-dealer on a kill has ≤14 hours stamina remaining, that monster drops no loot.
- [server] Stamina regen while logged out: +1 minute stamina per 3 minutes offline, up to the 39-hour mark; for the final 3 hours of the pool, 6 minutes offline are needed per 1 minute regained.
- [server] Stamina regen only starts after 10 continuous minutes logged off (no regen in the first 10 minutes offline).
- [server] Premium players with sufficient daily reward streak can regen stamina/soul in a "resting area" (see 5.1.10 Resting Area condition) while online.

## 5.1.5 Skills

- [server] 8 skills total: Fist Fighting, Club Fighting, Sword Fighting, Axe Fighting, Distance Fighting, Shielding, Fishing, Magic Level.
- [server] Skills do not scale with level; they increase through use (using the matching weapon type trains the matching skill).
- [server] Offline training: logging out next to a training statue, or (if owned) selecting a skill to train while sleeping in a personal bed, trains that skill passively while offline.
- [client] Skill-up triggers a white system message; skill level bar (visible per-skill) fills toward next point.
- [server] Fist Fighting: used when no weapon equipped (bare-handed damage), generally weak.
- [server] Club Fighting: blunt weapons (clubs, maces, staves, hammers).
- [server] Sword Fighting: edged weapons (knives through great swords).
- [server] Axe Fighting: axes (hatchets to top-tier) and pole weapons (halberds, lances).
- [server] Distance Fighting: thrown weapons (stones, spears) plus bows/crossbows.
- [server] Shielding: chance to block attacks while wielding a shield; hard cap of blocking at most 2 simultaneous opponents (also stated in combat 5.3.1 and 5.3.8).
- [server] Fishing: requires fishing rod + worms in inventory, used on a water tile that contains fish; catch chance scales with fishing skill; each attempt (success) consumes one worm; not all water tiles have fish.
- [server] Magic Level: scales spell power (damage and healing) similarly to a weapon skill; a low-level caster with high magic level can out-damage/out-heal a higher-level caster with lower magic level.

## 5.1.6 Promotion

- [server] Each vocation has one elite/promoted rank, granted by an NPC (king of Thais, queen of Carlin, dwarven emperor of Kazordoon, grand vizier of Ankrahmun) after the player asks to be promoted.
- [server] Promotion requirements: level ≥ 20; one-time fee of 20,000 gp; account must be Premium.
- [both] Promotion benefits: title change (e.g. paladin → "royal paladin", sorcerer → "master sorcerer"); faster HP/mana regen (druids/sorcerers: faster mana regen; knights: faster HP regen; paladins/monks: both faster); soul cap 200 + 15s soul tick (duplicates 5.1.4); reduced XP/skill loss on death (see 5.1.11); one extra vocation-specific spell unlocked; access to combat "stances" (special behavior-modifying modes).
- [server] If Premium lapses, all promotions on the account are suspended (character reverts to normal rank, all promo benefits off) without losing the promoted status permanently; restoring Premium restores promoted status with no new fee.

## 5.1.7 Wheel of Destiny

- [server] From level 51, promoted characters on Premium accounts earn 1 promotion point per level.
- [server] Points can be spent anytime/anywhere online on: max mana, max HP, or capacity (low-level slices), or on new/enhanced spells (higher-level slices).
- [server] Removing points or resetting the Wheel requires being at a temple.
- [server] Structure: 4 domains × 9 slices = 36 total slices holding perks.
- [server] Perk tiers: Dedication (small, scaling per point invested, capped per slice), Conviction (medium/large; unlocked once a slice is fully filled; effect stacks across multiple copies of the same Conviction perk across the wheel), Revelation (powerful; exactly 4 total, one per domain; unlocked by cumulative points spent in that domain; stage 2/3 unlock at higher spend in-domain).
- [server] Progression starts from a fixed center with 4 selectable initial slices; a slice must be filled completely before adjacent slices unlock — tree/graph-shaped unlock dependency, branching outward.
- [server] Each vocation has its own unique wheel layout/spells, with some perks shared across vocations.
- **Gem Atelier**
- [server] Gems drop from "fiendish" creatures and select bosses; must be revealed (spent gold) to gain mods.
- [server] Gem sizes: Lesser (1 mod), Regular (2 mods), Greater (3 mods) — mods assigned randomly on reveal; higher size = higher reveal cost.
- [server] Gems are vocation-locked to reveal: Guardian=knight, Marksman=paladin, Spiritualist=monk, Sage=sorcerer, Mystic=druid; only a character of the matching vocation can reveal a gem of that type; unrevealed gems of other vocations are tradeable, revealed gems are not.
- [server] Each revealed gem also has a domain colour (one of the 4 wheel domains); "Switch Domain" moves it one domain clockwise per use, for a fee, repeatable.
- [server] Gems socket into "vessels"; placing/removing via "Place in Vessel" / "Remove from Vessel".
- [server] Number of active mods on a placed gem = number of "Vessel Resonance" perks completed for that domain (3 such perks per domain; 1 perk active = 1 mod active, up to all 3 = fully active greater gem). Mods beyond unlocked resonance perks stay dormant.
- [server] Gems can be locked (prevents domain switch/dismantle) via a lock icon.
- [server] Dismantle converts a revealed gem into fragments; disallowed if locked, in a vessel, or if it's the last gem of its domain/colour.
- **Fragment Workshop**
- [server] Upgrades gem mods Grade I→IV using gold + fragments (lesser/greater fragment types).
- [server] Fragment yield depends on gem quality and reveal state: Lesser unrevealed 1–2, Lesser revealed 1–3 (lesser fragments); Regular unrevealed 2–4, Regular revealed 2–5 (lesser fragments); Greater unrevealed 1–2, Greater revealed 1–3 (greater fragments). Source: crushing unrevealed gems or dismantling revealed ones.
- [server] Fragments are tradeable and purchasable from jewelry NPCs.
- [server] Basic mods cost lesser fragments; supreme mods cost greater fragments.
- [server] Cooldown-augmentation mods don't reduce cooldowns directly — they add a chance to gain "Momentum" instead.
- [server] Fully enhancing any mod to Grade IV grants +1 permanent promotion point (once per fully-enhanced mod across the whole wheel).
- [server] Mod grade is shared per mod-type across all of a character's gems, capped by the lowest grade of any "preceding" mod on that gem chain (UI shows a limiter icon/tooltip when capped).
- **Presets / Planner**
- [client] Presets let players save/swap whole Wheel configs while at a temple; import/export via code or URL is supported (shareable across characters).
- [client] "Points" column in preset UI shows points available for that preset.
- [client] External Wheel of Destiny Planner tool on the Tibia website: lets players simulate vocation/point allocations and see required point costs; import/export supported.
- [server] All Wheel of Destiny benefits are suspended if Premium lapses (like promotion), restored on Premium renewal.

## 5.1.8 Equipment: Imbuing, Fusion and Tier Transfer

**a) Imbuing**
- [server] Imbuing (temporary equipment bonus) requires completing the "Temple of the Forgotten Knowledge" quest (delivering required tomes) to imbue via a shrine directly.
- [server] Items support up to 3 imbuement slots.
- [server] Imbuing requires an imbuing shrine (also purchasable by house owners via Store for home placement); item must be unequipped to imbue.
- [server] Premium players choosely from 3 imbuement power tiers: basic, intricate, powerful. Each imbuement costs gold + specific "astral source" materials (must be in backpack/stash).
- [server] Free-account / non-quest-complete players can still apply intricate/powerful imbuements via a scroll consumable, usable by anyone; only "worthy" Premium characters can produce/buy blank scrolls (from NPC Albinius); scrolls are also lootable.
- [server] Blank scroll → imbuement scroll conversion: done at a shrine via "Imbue Scroll" option, consuming gold + ingredients for chosen imbuement.
- [server] Imbuement scrolls apply directly from inventory onto the target item.
- [server] Imbuement duration: 20 hours of "active hunting equipped" time (countdown only decreases while the imbued item is equipped and the character is hunting, not real-time).
- [server] Imbuement can be cleared early for a fee via shrine or an "etcher" device (also sold by Albinius to worthy characters).

**b) Fusion and Tier Transfer**
- [server] Weapons, armor, boots, legs, helmets (depending on classification) can reach tier 1–10.
- [server] Tier effect per slot: Weapon = chance of bonus damage; Armor = chance to fully negate an attack's damage; Helmet = chance to reduce all cooldowns during combat; Legs = chance to transform into vocation avatar for 7s on weapon/offensive rune/offensive spell use (avatar form: damage reduction + guaranteed crits with extra crit damage); Boots = increases proc chance of other tiered items worn.
- [server] Higher tier = higher proc chance for these effects (no numeric curve given).
- [server] Tiers gained via Fusion or Tier Transfer at the Exaltation Forge (north of Adventurers' Guild); requires gold + resources obtainable from special monsters (Premium-only), findable via the "Find Fiend" spell.
- [server] Fusion: combines 2 items of identical name + identical tier, neither imbued, plus extra ingredients, to raise one item's tier by 1. On success: source item consumed, target item's tier +1. On failure: target item unaffected, source item's tier reduced by 1 (or fully consumed if it had tier 0).
- [server] Fusion boosters: up to 2 "exaltation cores" spendable per fusion attempt — one raises success chance, one grants a chance to preserve the consumed item's tier despite failure/consumption.
- [server] Convergence Fusion (classification-4 items only): fuses multiple same-body-slot, same-tier items; costs significantly more; guaranteed success; cannot trigger the normal bonus proc chance boost from cores.
- [server] Tier Transfer: needs 2 same-classification items + exalted dust + an exalted core; neither item may be imbued; source item needs tier ≥2 (consumed); target item needs tier 0, receives (source tier − 1).
- [server] Convergence Transfer (classification-4 only): removes the −1 tier loss on transfer, at significantly higher gold cost.

## 5.1.9 Vocations

- [server] 5 vocations, selectable once a character reaches level 8: Druid, Knight, Monk, Paladin, Sorcerer.
- [server] Druid: pure caster, weak melee, strong earth/ice magic, best healer (sole source of "Ultimate Healing" rune recipe), good at making money via selling runes.
- [server] Knight: melee tank, strong shielding/HP, weak magic (basic spells only), strong carry capacity/gold generation, easiest vocation to level per manual's framing.
- [server] Monk: fist-fighting melee dual-wielder with elemental-bond weapon spells and mantra-based defense (see combat 5.3.3); versatile, hybrid melee/caster via Harmony/Virtue system (5.1.3).
- [server] Paladin: distance-fighting specialist (best ranged), decent melee/shielding (second only to knight), moderate magic access via wide rune usage, sole vocation with holy spells (strong vs undead); framed as the all-rounder.
- [server] Sorcerer: pure offensive caster, weak melee, strongest raw magic damage potential, specializes energy/fire, sole source of "Sudden Death" rune and "Hell's Core" spell.

## 5.1.10 Special Conditions

- [client] Conditions display as up to 6 icons (configurable: inline bar below inventory, or beside the character in-world); a 7th+ condition collapses into a "..." overflow icon. Configurable via Options → Interface → HUD.
- [server] All special conditions are temporary and self-expire eventually.
- [server] Agony: continuous damage-over-time; cannot be cured, blocked, or resisted; must be waited out.
- [server] Bakragore's Taint: up to 5 stacking taint levels from repeated exposure in Bakragore's lairs. Penalties scale by stack: (1) some melee creatures can swap positions with nearby characters, (2) on monster death a stronger creature may spawn from the corpse, (3) monsters gain extra abilities, (4) characters take increased damage from all sources; 5th taint (from defeating Bakragore) removes penalties and instead grants boosted loot/XP plus essence drops from his "progeny". Party taint level = lowest taint among party members; gaining a taint requires matching that minimum, not already having the boss's current taint, and having dealt damage in the fight. Higher taint improves loot/XP.
- [server] Bleeding: DoT HP loss over time; cured instantly by "Cure Bleeding" spell.
- [server] Burning (fire damage type): DoT with periodic visible flare, magnitude depends on the source that caused it; curable by druids ("cure burning"-type spell).
- [server] Cursed (death damage type): periodic HP loss at an increasing rate over time; only paladins can cure (holy magic).
- [server] Dazzled (holy damage type): periodic HP loss at a decreasing rate, fixed number of ticks; no cure spell — must wait out or be healed by another player (no dedicated cure spell named).
- [server] Drunk: movement becomes erratic (walking control impaired); caused by drinking Tibian beer.
- [server] Drowning: HP loss while underwater without correct equipment (diving helmet prevents); only remedy is leaving water or equipping the helmet.
- [server] Electrified (energy damage type): periodic HP loss, similar mechanic to burning; only druids can cure via "Cure Electrification" spell.
- [server] Feared: lose control of character (forced to flee the fearing creature); cannot cast spells or use items while feared.
- [server] Freezing (ice damage type): periodic HP loss over an extended duration, caused by certain monsters' freezing breath; no cure spell, but a priest can heal HP loss (not remove condition, implied only HP restore).
- [server] Goshnar's Taint: up to 5 stacking penalty levels from Goshnar boss kills in his lair: (1) chance a monster teleports near you, (2) small chance a new creature spawns when you hit something, (3) increased damage received, (4) moderate chance a creature fully self-heals instead of dying, (5) lose 10% of current HP and mana every 5 seconds.
- [server] Haste: movement speed boost (opposite of Slowed); does not speed up HP/mana regen or attack rate; from spells or magic items.
- [server] Hexed: 3 severities — Lesser (reduced healing received), Intense (reduced healing received + reduced damage dealt), Greater (adds reduced max/current HP on top of Intense's effects). Source: certain boss monsters.
- [server] Hungry: slower HP/mana regeneration; cured by eating any food item.
- [client] Logout Block: icon shown whenever violence-related; see combat 5.3.12.c for full mechanics.
- [server] Magic Shield: incoming HP-loss damage is instead deducted from mana while the shield lasts; once mana hits 0, further damage hits HP normally.
- [server] Poisoned (earth damage type): DoT, magnitude varies by source; cured by "Cure Poison" spell or "Cure Poison Rune".
- [server] Powerless: cannot cast attack spells or use offensive runes.
- [server] Protection Zone: marks standing in a PZ; see combat 5.3.7 for full rules.
- [server] Protection Zone Block: always paired with a logout block; prevents entering PZs after attacking a non-party target first; not triggered by attacking your own party members; see combat 5.3.12.c.
- [server] Resting Area: subset of protection areas (houses, temples, depots); safe like a PZ; a second distinct icon variant marks characters with daily reward streak ≥2, who get an added resting bonus (e.g. faster HP/mana regen) while resting there.
- [server] Rooted: cannot move for a few seconds; cannot be removed/cured/dispelled.
- [server] Slowed: reduced walking speed until expiry or dispelled by healing magic; other actions (e.g. spellcasting) unaffected.
- [server] Strengthened: temporary skill/stat buff from various spells; vocation-dependent effect (e.g. magic level up, HP regen up, weapon skill up, shielding up, or boosting a party member's primary role) — commonly seen from party support spells.

## 5.1.11 Death

- [server] 0 HP = death; character always respawns safely at home temple (character is effectively immortal, only "temporarily" dies).
- **Experience Loss**
- [server] Levels ≤23: lose 10% of total XP on death.
- [server] Levels ≥24: loss framed as levels lost rather than % — scales down gradually; every 50 levels the loss increases by half a level, starting at losing 1 level at level 50 (examples given: level 100 → 1.5 levels lost, level 150 → 2 levels, level 180 → 2.3 levels, level 200 → 2.5 levels). [server: exact numbers given only as worked examples, not a closed formula]
- [server] Promoted characters get a flat 30% reduction off the computed XP loss.
- [server] Blessings each reduce XP loss by 8% (6.31% on Retro Hardcore PvP worlds); 7 blessings + promoted = up to 86% total reduction (74.17% on Retro Hardcore PvP).
- **Skill Loss**
- [server] Skills also regress on death; because of how skill-level progress bars work, a character close to a skill-up may not actually lose a skill level (just accumulated progress), so effective loss is progress-bar-dependent, not a flat skill-level decrement.
- [server] Promotion and blessings reduce skill loss the same way they reduce XP loss (qualitative statement, no separate numbers given).
- **Item Loss and Blessings**
- [server] Each equipped item independently has a 10% chance to drop on death; bags/backpacks (with contents) always drop unless prevented by blessings/Amulet of Loss.
- [server] Blessing item-loss-protection scaling: 1 blessing=30%, 2=55%, 3=75%, 4=90%, 5+=100% protection against losing equipment/containers.
- [server] "Amulet of Loss" fully protects equipped/character-slot items on death (for characters with <5 blessings) unless the character has a red or black skull.
- [server] Red/black-skulled characters always lose all equipment on death regardless of blessings, and always lose PvE blessings even if the death was a PvP death (PvP blessing doesn't help them either).
- [server] PvP Blessing: separate purchasable blessing protecting regular blessings specifically in PvP deaths. A death counts as "PvP" if ≥5% of damage taken in the last 5 minutes was PvP damage, OR the fatal blow was dealt by a character/summon. 5+ regular blessings = full equip protection even in PvP. Owning PvP blessing but no regular blessings/Amulet of Loss still saves the PvP blessing itself from being lost. Red/black skulled characters get no benefit from PvP blessing but also don't lose it on death. Price: same as one regular blessing at temples, up to level 120; price continues rising with level up to 270 (unlike flat regular-blessing pricing).
- [server] Adventurer's Blessing: automatic free protection for characters ≤ level 20 on open-PvP worlds; shown via an ankh icon + yellow-tinted inventory; protects fully (no item/XP/skill loss) if killed in PvP while active; lost permanently the moment the character either attacks another character first, or reaches level 21.
- [server] Dropped items remain on the corpse, lootable by anyone; if backpack/bag itself was lost, character receives a fresh empty bag afterward.
- [server] Characters level ≤8 never lose items on death (hard floor).
- [server] Death Redemption (Store purchase): restores the XP/skill points that would have been saved had the character owned all regular blessings at time of death; purchasable only within 24h of death, only for the 3 most recent deaths, and not available on Retro Hardcore worlds.
- [server] Reduced-loss rule: losses (items/skills/XP) are lowered if the death was a PvP death (same 5%-damage-or-final-blow definition as above) where the sum of attacker levels exceeded the victim's own level; guildmates you're at war with are excluded from that attacker-level sum.
