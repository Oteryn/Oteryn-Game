Source: `docs/architecture/reviews/OTERYN_GAME_CRYSTAL_REAUDIT_2026-10-28.md`,
all 137 DONE/PARTIAL rows (58 DONE, 79 PARTIAL). The audit is read-only.
MISSING/OWNER-EXCLUDED/OUT-OF-SCOPE rows are outside this matrix; their absence
is not a claim that those mechanics are tested or implemented.

**No full mechanic row is qualified by this change.** Existing phases below
are bounded slices, often queries, refusals or conditional actions. They do
not prove every sub-action, reconnect durability or client presentation.
`UNTRACED` identifies work still required: owner citations are copied from the
audit for navigation and are **not** server entry-point evidence. No keyword
match, audit DONE label, unavailable capability or lack of starter inventory
is promoted into PASS, content SKIPPED or MISSING-SERVER.

The new character phase covers turn command results without relocation,
say/whisper echoes, and a non-spawn step followed by accepted logout and a
fresh admission at the saved position. It does not prove visible facing,
every floor transition, follow, yell, range boundaries or mute. A separate
floor phase walks an automatic floor change, relogs at the landing and walks
back; the production bundle-loader smoke checks both routes. Chat is
transient: reconnect must preserve position, not replay historical speech.
The quest slice checks a list query and its stability across fresh admission;
it does not start/advance a quest. The raw quest snapshot/delta payloads can
be replayed by the native client-runtime reducer. These are domain payloads,
not a complete captured Foundation/TLS transcript.

`<id>.full` is the required complete scenario for the runner. It deliberately
remains FAIL until its whole row has evidence. Additional bounded scenario
results are printed separately. Planned scenarios are not implemented cases.
Content marked UNRESOLVED needs a bundle lookup and a reachable route, not a
synthetic grant or a manufactured database item. Client replay gaps remain
explicit. Do not change these labels without tracing and executing the case.
